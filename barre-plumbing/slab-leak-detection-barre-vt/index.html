    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Barre, VT

    Cold water slab leaks do not create warm spots, so they are more difficult to see. They often show up as a running water meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards.     Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Barre

    Our team uses electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak.
