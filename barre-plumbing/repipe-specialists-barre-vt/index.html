    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Barre

    Slab leaks are a costly and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Clay soils in river valleys drain poorly and can hold water against foundations, so sump pumps are common in low-lying homes. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the damaged pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Town water systems in Barre and other communities are generally reliable, but older service lines can add sediment and discolored water.
