    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Many homes built from the 1980s through the early 2000s have one or the other.     Slab Leak Repiping in Barre

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Most Vermont homes have full basements, many with fieldstone or older foundations that can let in water during wet seasons. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement for Older Homes in Barre, VT

    Older homes may still have galvanized steel pipe that rusts from the inside out. Weak flow and rusty water are common. We remove or abandon the galvanized lines and replace them with PEX or copper, and your water pressure and water quality usually improve the same day the new lines go into service.
