    Commercial Disposal Repair in Barre

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and usually include a control box and a solenoid valve for water. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Barre, VT

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. It is not a repairable part.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Barre

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Barre, VT

    Jams and minor leaks usually cost far less than a new unit. Once a unit is eight to ten years old, leaking from the body or losing its motor, replacement usually wins, and we price both when it is a close call. You make the call.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Barre, VT

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Barre, VT

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Most repairs use parts from the truck. Motors on these units are not sold separately.
