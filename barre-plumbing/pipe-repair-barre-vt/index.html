    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Barre, VT

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper pins. Copper thins from the inside and develops pinholes, while PEX usually fails at fittings or where it was kinked during installation, and our plumbers carry soldering, press and crimp tools so they can repair either one with the right connection. Each repair is pressure tested.

    Pinhole Leak Repair in Barre

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it. High velocity, poor grounding and aggressive water all speed it up, and the leak starts as a tiny drip that stains drywall or a ceiling, which we fix by replacing that length with new pipe. One usually means more are coming.

    Business Pipe Repair in Barre, VT

    Pipe trouble in a commercial building can close a business for the day. We keep you open. Our plumbers handle restroom groups, kitchen supply lines, mechanical room piping and larger diameter mains, scheduling the repair overnight or early morning when needed. Documentation is available.

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Barre

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Frozen water cracks it. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.

    Slab Leak Pipe Repair in Barre, VT

    Slab leaks start when pipe under the concrete wears through or gets stressed by soil movement. Rocky glacial soil and ledge are common across Vermont, which can make digging for water lines, sewer repairs or septic work slow and costly. Warm floors are a clue. We isolate the leaking line with pressure tests and find it with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. Both options get a price.

    PEX Pipe Repair and Installation in Barre

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, so we replace the failed joint with the right fitting for that brand of PEX. Most take under an hour.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Barre

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Flow drops slowly. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, which makes full replacement of the affected run the smarter spend. Transitions get dielectric fittings.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in Barre, VT

    A single pipe repair costs far less than a whole-house repipe. It makes sense on sound pipe. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can pass the cost of repiping within a few years, so we lay out both options with real numbers and tell you which one we would pick for our own house. The decision is yours.
