    What We Fix on Faucets in Barre, VT

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Barre

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. We replace what failed and check the rest. Compression, cartridge, ball and disc faucets all fail differently, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Barre

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. A new pop-up assembly goes in, matched to your faucet finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Barre

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Barre, VT

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Barre, VT

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Barre, VT

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. We carry commercial cartridges. Metering faucets get timed so they run long enough to rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Barre, VT

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Barre

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. If the discs are scratched, the whole cartridge gets replaced, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.
