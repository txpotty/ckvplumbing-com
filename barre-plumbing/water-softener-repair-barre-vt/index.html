    Water Softener Leak Detection & Repair in Barre, VT

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. Even a slow drip wastes water and soaks the floor. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. We test it under pressure.

    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Barre

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. We test the resin, clean it with a resin cleaner or replace it. Fresh resin brings capacity back.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Barre

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Hard water and chlorine wear out the seals, spacers and pistons. Leaks at the valve or water running to the drain are common signs. The valve gets rebuilt with the kit made for it.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Barre

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. After breaking up the crust, we clear the sludge and test the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Barre

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Barre

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.
