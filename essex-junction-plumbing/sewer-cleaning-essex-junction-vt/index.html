    Dealing with Grease Buildup in Essex Junction, VT

    Grease accumulation is a frequent cause of sewer line clogs, particularly from kitchen drains. Over time, grease solidifies and narrows the pipe, leading to slow drains and backups.     Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Essex Junction

    Older properties may still have vitrified clay or even Orangeburg fiber pipe running to the street. Clay joints are open invitations for roots, and Orangeburg can flatten under soil weight. We clean these lines carefully and use the camera to map joints and soft spots. If the pipe is failing, we will tell you plainly and show the footage. Cleaning keeps you running while you plan a replacement.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Essex Junction, VT

    Many older homes have cast iron drains under the slab or floor. Renovations in Essex Junction frequently mix original plumbing with newer copper or PEX, so a thorough look at transitions and fittings is important. Over time the inside of cast iron corrodes, scales and develops a channel along the bottom. Aggressive cutters can break thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting suited to older iron. This restores flow and gives a clear view on camera of the remaining wall. It also helps you decide if lining is a good option.
