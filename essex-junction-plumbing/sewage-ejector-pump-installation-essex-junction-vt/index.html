    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Essex Junction

    Residential ejector systems in Essex Junction serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer.     Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Essex Junction

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Essex Junction
