    Pressure Switch Replacement in Essex Junction

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, clogged sensing tubes and insect damage are frequent causes of failure. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank. This is one of the most budget-friendly well repairs.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Essex Junction

    The pressure tank works with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. Issues like a waterlogged tank, sudden pressure drops, or continuous pump operation often point to a faulty pressure tank. We inspect the air bladder, pressure switch, and tank connections to find the problem. In Essex Junction, we repair or replace tanks to optimize your well system's performance and extend pump life.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Essex Junction, VT

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. CKV Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Essex Junction operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.
