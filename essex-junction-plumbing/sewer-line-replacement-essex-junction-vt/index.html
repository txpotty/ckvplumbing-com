    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Essex Junction, VT

    To minimize disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement swaps out pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outward. This is ideal when protecting landscaping or driveways is a priority. The method works well for many homes in Essex Junction and can often be finished faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe known for its long life.

    Clay and Orangeburg Line Replacement in Essex Junction, VT

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, and Orangeburg fiber pipe flattens and blisters with age. Both are near the end of their life in many older properties. When the camera shows multiple failures, replacement is usually the best value. We remove or burst the old line and install modern pipe with a long expected service life. You get a smooth, root-resistant line out to the main.

    Sewer Pipe Lining as an Alternative in Essex Junction

    Cured-in-place pipe lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. After cleaning, a resin-soaked liner is pulled in and cured in place. This works when the host pipe still holds its shape without major sags or collapse. Lining can be a good option under slabs or finished areas. We tell you honestly if your line is a candidate.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Essex Junction, VT

    Traditional excavation is sometimes the best option, especially when the old line has a severe belly or the slope must be corrected. We dig along the line, remove the old pipe and set new PVC on compacted bedding at the correct grade. Rocky or hard-packed soil can slow digging, and we plan for that in the estimate. Each joint is glued and checked before backfill. The inspector sees the open trench before we cover it.

    Partial Sewer Line Repair in Essex Junction, VT

    Not every damaged sewer needs full replacement. If the camera shows a single break or one root-damaged joint, a spot repair may solve the problem. We locate the exact spot and depth, excavate a small area and replace that section. This costs far less than replacing the whole lateral. We only recommend it when the rest of the line is in sound condition.
