    Every Kind of Sink We Install in Essex Junction, VT

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Essex Junction, VT

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. Since the trap and stops are right behind the column, we center the drain and use chrome parts that look finished. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Vessel Sink Installation in Essex Junction, VT

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Commercial Sink Installation in Essex Junction, VT

    Commercial kitchens need sinks the health inspector will sign off on. Three-compartment sinks usually drain indirectly through an air gap to a floor sink, and hand sinks need tempered water within easy reach of every prep station on the line. Plan review comments come first. Mop basins and grease interceptor ties are part of it.

    Sink Installation Cost Comparison in Essex Junction, VT

    Drop-in swaps cost the least. An undermount on new stone costs more because of clips, cure time and a second trip to connect the plumbing, and a vessel sink adds a new drain and often a taller or wall-mounted faucet. Relocating the drain is the biggest add. We price each piece on the quote.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Essex Junction

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Old basket strainers fuse to the bowl, the disposal has to come off its mounting ring, and the dishwasher drain hose needs to be rerouted to the new disposal or tailpiece with a high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Essex Junction, VT

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. That does not work on undermounts. Those get plumbed from below.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Essex Junction

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Drywall anchors do not cut it. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Wall-Mount and ADA Sink Installation in Essex Junction, VT

    Wall-hung lavatories need a carrier or heavy blocking. ADA layouts call for a rim at or under 34 inches, open knee space, and insulated pipes below. An offset tailpiece frees up knee room. Lever or sensor faucets finish it off.
