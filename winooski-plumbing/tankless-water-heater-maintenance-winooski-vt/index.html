    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Winooski

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. We replace spent media. An outdoor run of condensate line gets checked for support and protection.

    Heat Exchanger Descaling in Winooski

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is the exchanger. Minerals settle on the hottest tube walls first, and the passages get narrower. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Noritz Tankless Tune-Ups in Winooski, VT

    Noritz builds a solid heater, but solid does not mean maintenance-free. A Noritz tune-up starts at the exhaust, where we look for soot and blocked terminations. Then we check the fan and the combustion air path. Last, we check the temperature setting and run a few fixtures at once, which shows whether the unit holds temperature between back-to-back showers.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Winooski, VT

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. We take a flow and temperature reading on each heater. Then we compare them.

    Noritz Descaling in Winooski, VT

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.

    Annual Tankless Checkup in Winooski, VT

    The annual checkup is a top-to-bottom pass on the heater. We read gas pressure at the inlet with a manometer while the burner is at full fire and compare it to the rating plate. We check the flue for blockages. A written report with every reading goes in your file for next year's comparison.
