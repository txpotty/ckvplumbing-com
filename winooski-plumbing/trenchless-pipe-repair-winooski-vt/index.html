    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Winooski

    CIPP lining is a proven trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Winooski.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Winooski

    We use proven trenchless technologies and brands like Perma-Liner for reliable pipe repair in Winooski.     Pipe Bursting Services in Winooski, VT

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, breaking apart the old pipe at the same time.
