    What Sewer Repair Covers in Winooski, VT

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Winooski

    Every repair starts with a look inside. We run the camera from the cleanout. You get a copy of the video and a plain written summary of each defect with its distance from the house, which helps if you are buying a home, filing a claim or getting a second opinion on a quote someone else gave you.     Sewer Pipe Replacement Options in Winooski, VT

    Replacement makes sense when the camera shows failures every few feet. Spot repairs will not keep up. We price open trench replacement, pipe bursting and full-length lining side by side for your Vermont property, showing what each method costs and what it does to the yard. Depth, access and what sits on top of the line decide it.

    Orangeburg and Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Winooski

    Orangeburg is wood fiber soaked in coal tar. It flattens with age. Cast iron from the same era rusts from the inside, builds scale and eventually splits along the bottom where the sewage runs, so by the time a camera shows widespread damage, spot repairs are a poor investment. PVC goes in, usually end to end. Trenchless bursting is often an option.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Winooski, VT

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. Fixing the joint treats the cause. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Winooski

    Some lines are too far gone for a liner. A fully collapsed section is one example. When the camera shows a collapse, a large offset or a belly deep enough to hold several inches of water, we open a trench, remove the old pipe and lay new Schedule 40 PVC on proper bedding at a quarter inch of fall per foot. Older homes often need this on at least one section. The dig is staged so the house is without a sewer for as little time as possible.

    Sewer Repair for Commercial Properties in Winooski, VT

    Commercial laterals run six inches or larger and carry far more flow than a house. A closed kitchen loses revenue by the hour. For restaurants, strip centers and apartment buildings in Winooski we schedule repairs after hours where we can, set up temporary bypass pumping when the line has to stay in service, and give managers a clear window to share with tenants. Grease trap outlet lines are one of our most common commercial repairs.

    Sewer Vent Pipe Repair in Winooski, VT

    A plugged vent makes drains gurgle and traps run dry. Sewer gas follows soon after. Bird nests, leaves and ice can block a roof vent, and older galvanized or cast iron vent stacks crack inside the walls where nobody sees them, which is why we look at the vent whenever the lateral checks out fine. We clear it from the roof or replace the damaged section.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Winooski

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.
