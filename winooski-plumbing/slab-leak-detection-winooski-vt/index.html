    How We Find Slab Leaks in Winooski, VT

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Winooski

    A lot of newer houses are built on post-tension slabs. These slabs have steel tendons stretched under tens of thousands of pounds of force, and cutting one by mistake is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We scan for tendons with ground-penetrating radar or a rebar locator before opening anything, or suggest a reroute that skips the slab.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Winooski, VT

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Winooski, VT

    A leaking hot line heats the concrete over it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Listening Through Concrete in Winooski

    Ground microphones are sensitive. That means appliances, HVAC fans and traffic all show up in the headphones, so our techs shut off what they can and learn to tune out the rest, which is a skill that takes years to get good at. A pressurized line gives the clearest sound in your Winooski home.

    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Winooski

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.
