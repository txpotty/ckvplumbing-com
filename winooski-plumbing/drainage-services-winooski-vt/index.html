    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Winooski

    Cost depends on whether it is a clog or a construction job. Clearing a fixture or main line is a few hundred dollars at most. Camera inspections, jetting and sewer repairs step up from there, and yard systems like French drains, channel drains or dry wells are priced by the length of drain and the amount of digging involved. You get a written quote first.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It works fast and costs less. Jetting scours the full pipe wall, lasting longer, but it costs more and needs sound pipe.

    Our cable machines come from Spartan Tool and RIDGID, and our cameras are RIDGID SeeSnake units with built-in locators. We run cart and trailer jetters. Good equipment matters because a camera with a locator tells us exactly where and how deep a problem is, which keeps a sewer repair to one small excavation instead of a long trench.

    Drain clearing carries a service guarantee for a set period. Repeat clogs get a return visit. If the camera shows broken or root-filled pipe, we tell you the clearing is temporary.

    Roots grow toward the water and nutrients inside sewer pipe. They only need a hairline gap. Once a root tip gets in through a cracked joint or loose seal, it grows into a mass that catches paper and grease, and clay and older concrete pipe with many joints are the most vulnerable.

    Snaking is the right first step for most single clogs. It costs less and takes less time. Jetting wins for grease, scale and recurring roots since it cleans the whole wall.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. A slow drain is the signal. Root-prone sewers and grease-heavy kitchens benefit from cleaning every one to two years.

    Installations like French drains, dry wells and sewer repairs carry a workmanship warranty. Drain clearing has a separate guarantee. If a drain we installed does not perform as promised, we fix it at no labor cost.
