    How We Replace Polybutylene in Winooski, VT

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Winooski

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. A burst fitting in an attic or wall can cause major damage in minutes. After an emergency repair we schedule the full replacement, since removing the poly system is the only lasting fix.

    Full Home Repiping with PEX in Winooski

    Most poly replacements we do use PEX-A, a flexible tubing that runs long distances with few fittings. On a slab home, we route the new lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, which keeps every supply line out of the concrete. PEX handles a freeze better than copper. Expansion fittings keep the bore full size, which helps pressure at the last fixture on the line.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Winooski, VT

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Faster install means lower labor cost. We keep PEX out of direct sunlight, away from water heater flues and recessed light housings and support it at the spacing the manufacturer calls for, which protects both the pipe and the warranty.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Winooski, VT

    Some homeowners in Winooski, VT want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    Polybutylene Leak Repair in Winooski

    Repairs on polybutylene are temporary by nature, because the pipe next to the repair is the same age. We still make them. We cut out the failed piece and splice in PEX with listed fittings, stopping the leak and giving you time to plan the full job.
