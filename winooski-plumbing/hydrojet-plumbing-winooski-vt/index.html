    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Winooski

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Winooski, VT

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Some Vermont wells carry iron, manganese or other naturally occurring minerals, so a water test is the first step before choosing treatment. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. You can see the change on video.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in Winooski, VT

    Retail and office buildings tend to have long drain runs where buildup settles. Flat runs collect more debris. We jet those runs on a schedule that matches the building's use, and we can work after hours so tenants are not dealing with equipment in hallways or restrooms during the day.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Winooski, VT

    Restaurant kitchen lines send warm grease downstream where it cools and sticks. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.

    Commercial Hydrojet Plumbing in Winooski, VT

    Businesses put more through their drains than any house. Multifamily and office buildings carry heavy daily loads. We jet stacks, laterals and mains for commercial buildings, often before opening hours, and we leave a written report with camera notes for the property manager's records.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Winooski

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. Camera findings set the interval. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Sewer Lateral Jetting in Winooski, VT

    The lateral from your house to the street handles every drain in the house, so buildup there causes backups everywhere at once. We jet it from the cleanout to the tap. Low spots where the pipe has settled hold water and collect sludge, so we spend extra passes there and mark the location on the camera footage in case a repair makes sense later.

    Emergency Hydrojet Services in Winooski, VT

    A full sewer backup cannot wait for a weekday slot. First, we open the line. Usually a cable gets the water moving, and then we jet the line to remove what caused the backup, whether that is a grease plug, a root mass or a buildup of wipes caught on a rough joint.
