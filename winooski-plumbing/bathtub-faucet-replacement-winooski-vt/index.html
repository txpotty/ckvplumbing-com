    Tub Spout Replacement in Winooski

    Spouts mount two ways. Slip-on spouts slide over a 1/2 inch copper stub and lock with a set screw underneath, while threaded spouts screw onto a 1/2 inch pipe nipple, and putting the wrong type on causes leaks behind the wall. We check yours before buying. Diverter spouts must match too.

    Replace or Keep Repairing in Winooski

    Repeated repairs add up. If a faucet has had three cartridges in five years, the valve body is likely worn where the cartridge seals, and replacing the whole valve once costs less than another round of service calls. Your service history helps us decide. Newer valves often come with lifetime cartridge warranties.

    Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Winooski, VT

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. On a single-handle setup the diverter is usually the lift gate in the spout itself, and on a three-handle faucet it is the middle stem, and both wear out until water splits between the spout and the head. Spout diverters are a quick swap. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Winooski

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. The supply lines come up through the floor under the filler, which means cutting the slab or working from a crawlspace or basement, and the filler needs a mounting bracket anchored to the subfloor. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.
