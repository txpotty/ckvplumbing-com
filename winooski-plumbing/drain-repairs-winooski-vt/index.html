    Exterior Drain Tile Repair in Winooski, VT

    Drain tile, sometimes called a French drain, carries groundwater away from the footing. It clogs with silt. Rocky glacial soil and ledge are common across Vermont, which can make digging for water lines, sewer repairs or septic work slow and costly. When a tile line is crushed, clogged or was laid without fabric, water builds up against the foundation, so we camera the tile, find the failed section and replace it with perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric and clean gravel. We confirm it drains to daylight or a sump.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Winooski

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. Water pools at the house. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and repair broken runs so downspout water gets carried away from the footing. The discharge point gets checked as well.
