    Trenchless Water Line Repair in Winooski

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Winooski

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. Most codes cap it at 80 psi.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Winooski

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. Straight runs are ideal. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.
