    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Winooski

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the different advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our experts in Winooski can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Winooski
