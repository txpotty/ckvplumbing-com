    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Shelburne, VT

    Modern WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons per flush or less, and dual-flush models offer an even lighter option for liquid waste.     Comfort-Height and ADA Toilet Installation in Shelburne

    Comfort-height toilets put the seat at about 17 to 19 inches, which is easier on knees and backs. For accessible bathrooms, we follow ADA guidelines for seat height, centerline distance from the side wall and grab bar clearance. We can install elongated bowls and add blocking for grab bars while the wall is open. These upgrades are popular with homeowners planning to age in place.

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Shelburne

    In new homes and additions, we set toilets after the finished floor is in so the flange sits at the right height. We flush-test and check for leaks before the final inspection.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Shelburne

    Pressure-assisted toilets provide a strong flush, ideal for commercial settings or homes with specific needs. Their installation differs from gravity-fed models, requiring careful attention to the pressure tank and water supply. We make sure all components are correctly fitted and calibrated for optimal performance. Our plumbers in Shelburne, VT are skilled in setting up these heavy-duty systems to prevent water hammer or excessive noise.

    A key part of any toilet installation is ensuring a proper seal between the toilet and the waste pipe, typically achieved with a wax ring and a secure flange.
