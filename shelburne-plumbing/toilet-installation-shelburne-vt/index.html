    Standard Toilet Installation in Shelburne

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out.     Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Shelburne, VT

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. Cast iron flanges rust through at the bolt slots. PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Shelburne, VT

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low.     Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Shelburne
