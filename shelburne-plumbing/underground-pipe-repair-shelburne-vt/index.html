    Pipe Bursting Services in Shelburne, VT

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Water Main Underground Repair in Shelburne

    Your water main is the pipe between the meter and the house. You own that part. When it breaks, we locate the leak without digging, expose the pipe and cut out the bad section and join in new pipe with buried-rated fittings. Rusted steel lines rarely patch well.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Shelburne

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, which is why we recommend a full swap to PEX or copper threaded along the existing route. Pressure comes back right away.
