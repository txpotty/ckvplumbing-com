    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Shelburne

    We offer installation for both PEX-A and PEX-B piping and understand the distinct advantages of each. PEX-A, often preferred for its flexibility and ability to recover from minor kinks, uses expansion fittings. PEX-B is more rigid but also very durable, typically using crimp or compression fittings. Our plumbers in Shelburne can help you decide which type of PEX suits your project, considering factors like budget and application.

    Signs you might need PEX repiping include persistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are typical in older Shelburne homes and point to a failing plumbing system.

    PEX offers several advantages over copper, including lower material and installation costs, greater flexibility and resistance to corrosion and freezing. Unlike copper, PEX does not form pinhole leaks over time due to water chemistry, making it a more durable choice for many Shelburne homes. It also cuts down on water hammer noise.

    Multiple pinhole leaks, discolored water, low pressure throughout the house and gray polybutylene pipe are typical signs. Recurring slab leaks are also a strong reason to repipe overhead.

    We install Uponor PEX-A along with Viega, Zurn and SharkBite PEX-B systems. We recommend the system that fits your project and budget.

    Small repairs are possible for handy homeowners, but a full repipe requires proper sizing, support spacing and code compliance. Mistakes behind walls can cause hidden leaks and failed inspections.
