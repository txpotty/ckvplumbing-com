    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Shelburne, VT

    Electric tank water heaters are common in homes without natural gas service. We install models with the correct voltage and breaker size for your electrical panel. These units do not need venting, which allows more flexible placement. We also install a drain pan when the heater is in a closet or attic. Electric models are simple, dependable and quiet.

    Expansion Tank Installation in Shelburne, VT

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Shelburne, VT

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are more and more popular in Vermont homes. Our plumbers specialize in installing these efficient units, ensuring proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed. We also address venting requirements, which are often different from traditional units. While the upfront cost can be higher, the long-term energy savings and endless hot water supply make them an attractive option for many homeowners in Shelburne. We'll help you choose the right model for your particular needs.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Shelburne

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Plumbing in Vermont follows state plumbing rules based on a national model code with state amendments, and the state licenses plumbers. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to ensure proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Shelburne. This small component offers real long-term protection.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Shelburne, VT

    Tank water heater installations usually cost less upfront than tankless systems. Gas tankless installs can cost more because of gas line upgrades and venting. Attic installs, relocations and code upgrades may add to the price. We provide a written quote that includes the unit, labor, permits and haul-away.     Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Shelburne

    The cost of water heater installation in Shelburne, VT usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. CKV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    We install Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, Navien, Rinnai and other trusted brands. We recommend models based on reliability, warranty and budget.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater installation involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity and water connections, all of which carry serious risks if not handled correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, gas explosions, electrical fires and carbon monoxide poisoning. It can also void your warranty, and local codes often require a licensed plumber for this work.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more economical choice. Newer models also offer significantly better energy efficiency.

    Many tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years, though hard water can shorten that. Regular flushing and anode rod checks help extend life.

    A traditional tank water heater usually lasts between 8 and 12 years with proper maintenance. Tankless water heaters often have a longer lifespan, ranging from 15 to 20 years, thanks to their durable components and on-demand operation.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.
