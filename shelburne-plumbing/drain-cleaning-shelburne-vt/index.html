    Hydro Jetting for Drains in Shelburne

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. A jetter sprays the full circumference of the pipe at high pressure. Cabling opens a path, but jetting strips the buildup off the walls. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Shelburne, VT

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A sectional cable is built to chop and pull a clog out. Jetting strips grease and scale off the walls and carries it out of the line, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.
