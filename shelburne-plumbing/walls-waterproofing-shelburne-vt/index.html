    Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Shelburne

    The cost of walls waterproofing in Shelburne, VT can vary a lot, typically ranging from $3,000 for interior crack sealing to $15,000 or more for extensive exterior foundation waterproofing with drainage systems. Factors like the size of the area, severity of water damage, chosen methods, and accessibility all affect the final price. We provide detailed, transparent quotes after a thorough inspection.

    Look for bubbling paint, soft drywall, stained baseboards and musty odors. White crust on block or stucco walls is another sign of moisture moving through. Loose tiles or cracked grout in a shower also suggest water is getting behind.

    We use a range of high-performance waterproofing membranes and coatings depending on the application and foundation type. These can include liquid asphalt-modified polyurethane, rubberized asphalt membranes, bentonite clay systems, and crystalline coatings. Brands often include trusted names known for their durability and effectiveness in preventing water penetration.

    Skilled DIYers can roll on liquid membranes, but mistakes at seams, corners and drains often lead to leaks. Shower pans also require correct slope and flood testing. Professional installation lowers the risk of hidden water damage.

    We commonly install Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard, Laticrete Hydro Ban and similar systems. The right choice depends on your shower design and tile. We follow each manufacturer's installation steps to keep the warranty valid.

    Shower remodels that change plumbing or drain locations often require a permit. Simple membrane replacement may not. We check local requirements and pull permits when needed.

    A shower wall waterproofing project usually takes two to four days, including drying time between coats. Exterior block sealing can often be completed in a day. Larger foundation jobs may take a few days.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, typically managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.
