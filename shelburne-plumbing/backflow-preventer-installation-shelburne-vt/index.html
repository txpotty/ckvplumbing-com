    For high-hazard applications in Shelburne, VT where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is usually required.     Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Shelburne, VT

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Shelburne, VT, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also check for proper clearance and drainage.

    Irrigation Backflow Preventer Installation in Shelburne

    Landscape irrigation is the most common reason homeowners need a backflow device. We install pressure vacuum breakers or double check assemblies based on local requirements and the design of your system. Above-ground installs are set at least 12 inches above the highest sprinkler head when a PVB is used.
