    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Shelburne

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Combustion air matters. We confirm a closet install has louvers or ducted air before we light the burner.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Shelburne

    Forty and fifty gallon gas tanks are the workhorse in most Vermont homes, and a leak at the bottom seam means the tank is finished. Newer tanks can run taller than the old one. We check clearance before we order. Plan on about two to three hours.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Shelburne

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape. The old tank gets drained and hauled off, and the new one goes on a pan. Every install gets new gas and water connectors. We soap-test the gas joints and check the draft hood with the burner lit.

    No Hot Water Calls in Shelburne

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. An old tank with a bad valve usually gets replaced.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Service in Shelburne, VT

    Rheem tanks and their Ruud twins share most parts, which helps when a gas valve fails a few years in. Either one works. Setup follows the factory manual, including the draft test.

    Large Capacity 75 Gallon Gas Heaters in Shelburne, VT

    Big families with soaking tubs often outgrow a 50 gallon tank and need a 75 gallon heater or a high-recovery 50 at around 65,000 to 75,000 BTU. Those burners need more gas. We size the gas line before installing, since an undersized line cuts recovery.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Replacement in Shelburne, VT

    Rheem Performance and Performance Platinum tanks cover most of the range we install, from basic 6-year models to 12-year tanks with better anodes. The longer warranty usually means a bigger anode. Choosing is simple.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Shelburne
