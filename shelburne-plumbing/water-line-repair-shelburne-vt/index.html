    Main Water Line Repair in Shelburne

    The main water line brings water from the public supply to your home. When this line leaks or breaks, it can cause significant water loss, foundation damage, and low water pressure. Our team specializes in diagnosing and repairing main water line issues, from pinpoint leaks to full pipe ruptures, so your home's primary water source is secure.     Trenchless Water Line Repair in Shelburne

    For some water line repairs, traditional trenching can be avoided.     Main Water Line Leak Repair in Shelburne

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Shelburne, VT

    Many homes built before the 1970s still have galvanized steel service lines that rust from the inside. Some Vermont wells carry iron, manganese or other naturally occurring minerals, so a lab test is the first step before choosing treatment. Hard water speeds up scale buildup, which chokes flow and causes rust-colored water. Patching galvanized pipe rarely lasts because the surrounding pipe is just as corroded. We usually recommend replacing the full run from meter to house with copper or PEX, which restores pressure and stops repeat leaks.
