    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in South Burlington, VT

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Look for wet insulation.     Tunneling for Foundation Leaks in South Burlington, VT

    Tunneling means digging under the slab from outside the house. Your flooring stays put. We dig a pit beside the foundation and hand-tunnel to the leak, which works well when the break sits under cabinets, a tiled shower or hardwood you do not want torn up. CKV Plumbing backfills and compacts the tunnel when the South Burlington, VT repair is done.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in South Burlington, VT

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It's usually the lowest bid. We saw a small opening, dig down to the pipe, replace the damaged section and pressure test it, then backfill in layers and patch the concrete flush with the floor around it. Your flooring contractor can finish the surface afterward.

    Advanced Leak Detection Technology in South Burlington, VT

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water leaving a pipe under four inches of concrete. It does most of the work. CKV Plumbing pairs it with a line tracer that follows the pipe route and a thermal camera that shows hot water spreading, which narrows the spot to a foot or two in most houses we test, in South Burlington, VT or anywhere else.

    Water Line Reroute in South Burlington

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. The floor stays whole. We usually run PEX because it bends around framing and does not corrode, and the drywall openings are cut small, often just a few squares at fixture walls, so patching goes fast.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in South Burlington

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs with steel cables under thousands of pounds of tension. A cut cable can snap hard. Before we open the floor, we scan with ground-penetrating radar to map the cables, and if the leak sits too close to a tendon, rerouting the line is the safer call.
