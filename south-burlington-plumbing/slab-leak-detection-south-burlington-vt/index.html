    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in South Burlington

    Hot water lines leak under slabs more often than cold lines because heat speeds up corrosion and causes expansion and contraction. Signs include warm spots on the floor, a water heater that runs nonstop and higher gas or electric bills. Thermal imaging shows the heat pattern spreading through tile or concrete. We confirm with a pressure test and acoustic listening before flagging the spot.

    Emergency Slab Leak Detection in South Burlington

    A sudden drop in water pressure, visible dampness or the sound of running water can indicate an emergency slab leak. CKV Plumbing offers emergency detection services in South Burlington to address urgent situations quickly. We focus on rapid response to limit water damage and prevent further structural compromise to your property.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in South Burlington

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which specific line is compromised. This systematic approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, ensuring an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in South Burlington, VT.

    Repiping After Multiple Slab Leaks in South Burlington, VT

    If your home has had two or more slab leaks, the remaining pipe is likely corroded too. Repiping replaces all supply lines with new PEX or copper run through the walls and attic. It is a bigger project, but it ends the cycle of repeat slab leaks. We can usually complete a full repipe in two to four days.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in South Burlington

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs that use steel cables under high tension. Cutting into one without care is dangerous and can damage the foundation. We locate the cables with scanning equipment before any repair access is opened. This lets us repair the leak safely or recommend a reroute that avoids the slab entirely.
