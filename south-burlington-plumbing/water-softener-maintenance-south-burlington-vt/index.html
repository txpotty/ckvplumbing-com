    Salt Bridge Removal and Brine Tank Cleaning in South Burlington, VT

    Humidity and cheap salt cause most salt bridges. A hard shell forms across the tank, and the salt underneath dissolves away, leaving a hollow. The softener keeps regenerating on schedule, but it draws plain water instead of brine, which leaves the resin without a recharge and hard water flows to every tap.

    Resin Cleaning and Regeneration Tuning in South Burlington, VT

    Iron is the most common resin fouler we see. It coats the beads orange and blocks the exchange sites. We feed a resin cleaner through the brine well, run a regeneration and retest, and if the capacity comes back we set the valve to regenerate more often so iron cannot load the bed again.

    Regeneration Programming and Hardness Testing in South Burlington, VT

    Plenty of softeners still run factory settings. The installer never changed them, or the household grew and nobody updated the valve. We enter your raw hardness and household size, then set a metered valve to regenerate by gallons used rather than on a fixed timer schedule.

    Resin Rebedding in South Burlington

    Chlorine and chloramine in treated water slowly swell and soften resin beads, and iron or sediment can coat them until capacity drops off. A resin cleaner is the first thing we try. If the bed is spent, we empty out the old resin and refill the tank with new high-capacity media, which runs far less than a new softener.
