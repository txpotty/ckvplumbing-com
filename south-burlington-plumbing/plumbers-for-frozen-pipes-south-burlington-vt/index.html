    Thawing an accessible frozen pipe in South Burlington usually costs $150 to $400. Burst pipe repairs typically run $250 to $800, with higher costs when drywall or ceilings must be opened. We give you pricing before we start.

    To prevent pipes from freezing in South Burlington, insulate all exposed pipes, especially those in unheated areas like crawl spaces, attics, and basements.     While any pipe can freeze, older galvanized steel or copper pipes in uninsulated areas are often more susceptible to bursting due to their rigid material.     Ice can split a pipe while frozen, but the leak only appears once the ice melts and water flows again. That is why we pressure test every thawed line. It catches hidden splits before they cause damage.

    New installations, water heater replacements and significant repairs often need a permit, especially in multi-unit and rental buildings.     Most thawing jobs take one to two hours. Burst pipe repairs usually take one to four hours depending on access. Larger freeze damage with multiple splits can take a full day.

    A frost-free hose bib has a long stem that shuts off water inside the warm part of the wall. Brands like Woodford and Prier make reliable models. They greatly reduce freeze damage at outdoor faucets.
