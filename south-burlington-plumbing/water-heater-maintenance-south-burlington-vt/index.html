    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in South Burlington, VT

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in South Burlington, VT. We check for gas leaks, proper venting of exhaust gases, and the function of the pilot light or igniter.     T&P Valve and Safety Testing in South Burlington, VT

    The temperature and pressure relief valve keeps your water heater from building unsafe pressure. We test it by lifting the lever and checking that it opens and reseats. A valve that leaks or will not open gets replaced. We also check that the discharge pipe runs to an approved location. Expansion tanks are checked for proper air charge where installed.

    Water Heater Leak Detection and Prevention in South Burlington, VT

    Leaks are a frequent issue that can lead to significant water damage if not caught early.     Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in South Burlington

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part in your water heater designed to corrode instead of the tank itself. Depending on your water chemistry, these rods can wear down faster than you might think. During maintenance, we inspect the anode rod's condition and recommend replacement if necessary. A healthy anode rod is key for preventing rust and extending the lifespan of your water heater tank, saving you from a costly full replacement.

    Gas Water Heater Burner and Venting Check in South Burlington

    Gas water heaters need a clean burner and a proper vent to run safely. We inspect the burner flame, clean the combustion chamber and check the thermocouple or flame sensor. The vent is checked for proper draft and secure connections. Blocked or loose vents can let carbon monoxide into your home. We also check the gas line and shutoff valve for leaks.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in South Burlington

    The anode rod is a sacrificial part that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in South Burlington, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's heavily depleted.
