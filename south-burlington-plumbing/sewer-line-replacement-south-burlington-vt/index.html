    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in South Burlington, VT

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. We fuse the HDPE into one continuous length on the lawn. Most bursts finish in a day. Restoration is two small holes instead of a sawcut slab.

    Emergency Sewer Collapse Repair in South Burlington

    Every drain in the house stops when a lateral collapses. The tech scopes the line, marks the break and lets you know whether the dig can start next day or has to wait on locates. Utility marking takes a few days in most areas. A temporary bypass can keep a bathroom working in the meantime.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in South Burlington, VT

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. The first visit gets a camera in the line so we know if it has caved in or only needs clearing to buy time. If it has collapsed, we start permit and locate requests that day. CKV Plumbing can often run a temporary bypass. With a bypass in place, the house can use a toilet and a sink until the new pipe goes in.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in South Burlington

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Where we can, the sod is cut and set aside before the digging starts. The inspector sees open pipe. Backfill goes in compacted lifts so the trench line does not sink into a ditch a year later.
