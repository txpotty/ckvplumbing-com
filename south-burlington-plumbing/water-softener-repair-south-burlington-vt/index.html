    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in South Burlington, VT

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. CKV Plumbing repairs these units in South Burlington, VT. The media wears out in a few years.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in South Burlington, VT

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in South Burlington

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in South Burlington

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in South Burlington, VT

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Keep the salt about half full.

    Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in South Burlington

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in South Burlington, VT

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.

    Resin Bed Replacement in South Burlington

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. CKV Plumbing pulls the old resin and fills the tank with new media. Capacity comes back right away.
