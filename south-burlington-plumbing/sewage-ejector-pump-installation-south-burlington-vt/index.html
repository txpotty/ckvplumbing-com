    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in South Burlington

    A high water alarm belongs on every ejector system, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. We install audible alarms with their own power source, or Wi-Fi alarms that text your phone, so you hear about a stuck float while the basin is still filling and not after waste has reached the basement floor. Severe summer storms have brought major flooding to valley towns, overwhelming drainage and pushing water back through sewer lines. Battery backup is an option.

    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in South Burlington, VT

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes often need duplex systems with an alternating control panel. The panel switches the lead pump each cycle so wear is shared, and if the lead pump fails or the flow is heavy, the second pump starts automatically and the panel trips an alarm so someone knows to call for service. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Grinder Pump Installation in South Burlington

    Grinder pumps chop waste into a fine slurry first, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. A grinder makes sense when the sewer is distant, uphill or pressurized, such as a pressure sewer where each home pumps into a shared main. Grinders run pricier. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in South Burlington, VT

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars send gray water down that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in South Burlington

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Ejector Pump Installation Cost Comparison in South Burlington, VT

    Replacing a pump in an existing basin is the least expensive option, often done in a few hours. A full new system with basin, floor excavation, roof vent, electrical and alarm costs more, but it is usually the only way to add a below-sewer bath besides a macerator. Ask for both numbers.
