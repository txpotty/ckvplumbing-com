    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in South Burlington

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in South Burlington, VT

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. Without needing a chimney, the vent can exit through a side wall within the allowed length. They need an outlet. We terminate the vent to spec.
