    Condensing Tankless Repair in St. Albans, VT

    Condensing tankless heaters pull extra heat out of the exhaust and make acidic condensate that has to drain somewhere. A plugged trap will lock the heater out. We clean the trap, refill the neutralizer and check the PVC vent for sag or the wrong slope.

    Igniter and Flame Rod Repair in St. Albans, VT
