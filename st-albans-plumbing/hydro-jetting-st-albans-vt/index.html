    Frequently Asked Questions About Hydro Jetting in St. Albans

    The cost of hydro jetting in St. Albans, VT typically ranges from $300 to $800, depending on the severity of the clog, the length and diameter of the pipe, and ease of access. More complex jobs, like main sewer line descaling or extensive root removal, may be on the higher end. We provide a transparent quote after an initial inspection.

    Drain snaking uses a cable with an auger that punches a hole through a blockage to restore some flow. Hydro jetting, however, uses high-pressure water to completely scour the inside of the pipe, removing all buildup and restoring the pipe to a near-new condition. Hydro jetting offers a more complete and longer-lasting clean.

    Most residential jobs take 1 to 3 hours including inspection. Long commercial lines or heavy grease can add time.

    No permit is needed for jetting. If the inspection shows damage that needs repair, permits may be required and we handle them.

    Snaking uses a rotating cable to break through a clog. Jetting uses pressurized water to clean the entire pipe wall and flush debris away.
