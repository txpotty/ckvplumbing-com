    Combination Sump Pump Systems in St. Albans

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in St. Albans

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. The pit lid sits flush. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in St. Albans, VT

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest.     Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in St. Albans, VT
