    Frequently Asked Questions About Bathtub Faucet Replacement in St. Albans

    Faucet repair involves fixing a specific component, like a washer or cartridge, to stop a leak or improve function. Replacement means installing an entirely new faucet fixture. We recommend replacement when the faucet is old, badly corroded, or has multiple issues that make repair uneconomical.

    Spout and trim swaps take about an hour. Cartridge replacement takes one to two hours, and a full valve replacement may take between three and six hours.

    Most standard bathtub faucet replacements can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring pipe modifications or involving older, corroded plumbing, might take longer. Our plumbers work efficiently to limit disruption.

    Our labor is guaranteed, and most major brands offer lifetime limited warranties on their valves and cartridges for residential use.
