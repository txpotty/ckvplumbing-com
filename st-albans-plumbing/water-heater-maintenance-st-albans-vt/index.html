    What a Water Heater Tune-Up Covers in St. Albans, VT

    Anode Rod Checks in St. Albans

    The anode rod rusts so the tank does not. Once the rod is eaten down to its core wire, the water goes after the tank itself. It comes out for inspection. A new anode costs far less than a new tank.

    Anode Rod Replacement in St. Albans

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. Spent rods get replaced. It keeps rust off the tank lining.

    Gas Burner and Venting Checks in St. Albans

    A blue flame is what we want to see. Yellow or lazy flames mean poor combustion, so we clean the burner and combustion chamber, check the thermocouple or flame sensor, and confirm the vent has a proper draft and tight connections. Bad venting is dangerous. We test the gas connection too.

    Maintenance for Every Brand in St. Albans

    Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, Noritz, Rinnai and more. Each brand has its own anode type, drain valve style and descaling procedure, and we follow the manufacturer's guidelines so the service holds up if you ever need to file a warranty claim. Model details matter. The truck carries common parts.

    Yearly Service Plans in St. Albans, VT

    Forgetting is the main reason tanks go unflushed. A yearly plan puts the visit on the calendar, sends a reminder a week ahead, and gives you priority scheduling and repair discounts if the heater ever acts up between visits. We track it for you. Long-term savings add up.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in St. Albans

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Electric Element Service in St. Albans, VT

    Two elements, two thermostats. Sediment coats the lower element first, it runs hot and burns out, and that is the most common reason an electric tank suddenly gives you only half a tank of hot water. Power gets shut off first. We only replace what failed.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in St. Albans, VT

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale inside the heat exchanger chokes flow until the unit throws codes or shuts off mid-shower. We flush it with descaler. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.
