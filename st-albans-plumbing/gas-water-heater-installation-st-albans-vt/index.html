    Propane Water Heater Installation in St. Albans

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Propane runs at a different pressure and needs a smaller burner orifice, so we install propane-specific models or convert units with the manufacturer kit where the manual allows it. We work with your supplier. Every joint is leak tested.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in St. Albans, VT

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We figure your household peak demand from bathrooms and people, then make sure the vent diameter, rise and total length match the manufacturer table and local code. A small vent can backdraft. Draft gets tested on every job.

    Gas Water Heater Permit and Inspection in St. Albans

    Gas water heater installs usually need a permit and a final inspection. CKV Plumbing pulls the permit and schedules the inspection in St. Albans. Inspectors look at vent connections, clearances, the relief line, the gas shutoff and the sediment trap, and we install all of them the way they expect to see them. Passing matters for insurance.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in St. Albans

    Atmospheric vent heaters rely on hot exhaust rising through a metal flue, with no fan. They are common in older homes and usually the least expensive gas option, but they only work if the vent has the right size, slope and clearance to combustibles. We inspect the existing vent. We replace bad sections.

    Cost-Effective Gas Water Heater Upgrades in St. Albans, VT

    Upgrading an old 0.55 UEF gas tank to a newer model can cut gas use noticeably. You get a side-by-side of efficiency, recovery and estimated yearly cost before you choose. Payback varies by use. Upkeep tips come with it.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in St. Albans, VT

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Because the exhaust comes out cool and wet, they vent through PVC and need a condensate drain, which changes where the heater can go and what the install involves. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Power Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in St. Albans, VT

    Power vent heaters use a blower to push exhaust through PVC or other approved pipe. That means the heater can go in a spot with no chimney or vertical flue, and the vent can run sideways out an exterior wall as long as it stays within the length limit. They need an outlet. We terminate the vent to spec.

    Rheem Gas Water Heater Installation in St. Albans, VT

    Rheem gas heaters show up in a lot of local homes, from basic atmospheric tanks to power vent models. CKV Plumbing installs the full Rheem line. We connect the gas, set the flue and check the Guardian flammable vapor sensor and the combustion air path so the safety features work as designed. Draft is checked last.
