    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in St. Albans

    A standard disposal replacement with the unit usually costs $275 to $650. Premium models like InSinkErator Evolution usually run $500 to $1,000 installed.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it usually safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.

    We install and replace all major brands, including InSinkErator, Moen, Waste King, KitchenAid, and Badger. Our plumbers are familiar with the installation requirements for each brand, for a proper fit.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to replace a garbage disposal, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, and voided warranties. For safety and peace of mind, hiring a professional plumber is the better choice.

    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's probably time for a replacement.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might suffice. Most homeowners opt for 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.
