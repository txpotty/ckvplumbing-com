    Frequently Asked Questions About Toilet Replacement in St. Albans

    We often recommend and install reliable brands like American Standard, Kohler, Toto, and Delta. These brands offer a range of durable, water-efficient models that perform well across a wide range of water conditions and are known for their long service life.

    Older toilets can use 3.5 to 7 gallons per flush, while WaterSense toilets use 1.28 gallons or less. For a average household, that can mean thousands of gallons saved each year.

    We recommend reliable brands known for their quality, efficiency, and availability of parts. Popular choices include Kohler, American Standard, Toto, Delta, and Gerber.     The 'rough-in' measurement is the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the drainpipe. Common rough-ins are 10, 12, or 14 inches. It is essential for selecting a new toilet that fits correctly without extensive changes to your bathroom plumbing.

    Signs of failing internal toilet parts include a toilet that runs constantly, a weak or incomplete flush, a handle that needs jiggling, or visible wear and tear on components like the flapper, fill valve, or flush valve. While some parts can be replaced individually, an older toilet with multiple failing components might benefit more from a full replacement.
