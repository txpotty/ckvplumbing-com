    Full Home Repiping with PEX in St. Albans

    When a home with polybutylene pipes sits on a slab foundation, repiping becomes a more complex process.     Polybutylene Identification and Inspection in St. Albans

    Polybutylene is usually gray, though it can be white, blue or black, and is often stamped PB2110. You may see it at the water heater, under sinks or near the main shutoff. We inspect visible lines, check the stamps and trace runs through the attic. You get a clear answer and a written estimate for replacement.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in St. Albans

    For homeowners who prefer a traditional, robust solution, copper piping is an excellent alternative to polybutylene. Copper is highly durable, resistant to bacteria growth, and has a proven track record of longevity. While typically a higher investment than PEX, copper offers superior strength and reliability. We provide careful copper pipe installation services across St. Albans.

    Copper Replacement Option in St. Albans

    Some homeowners choose Type L copper for its long history and rigid feel. We solder with lead-free solder or use pressed fittings for faster installation. Copper costs more than PEX and takes a little longer to install. We help you weigh the options for your budget and local water.

    Whole-Home PEX Replacement in St. Albans, VT

    PEX-A is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, scale resistant and easy to route. We use Uponor PEX-A with expansion fittings that do not restrict flow. New lines run from the main shutoff to every fixture, water heater and appliance. Most homes are completed in two to four days.
