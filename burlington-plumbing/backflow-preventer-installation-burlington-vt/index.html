    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Burlington

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a widely used backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Burlington, DCVAs are often installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer Installation in Burlington

    The cost for backflow preventer installation in Burlington, VT can range from about $300 to $1,500, depending on the type of device required, such as a DCVA or an RPZ, the complexity of the installation and any changes needed to existing plumbing. Commercial installations with larger devices may cost more. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after an on-site assessment.

    Most municipalities require annual testing of backflow preventers by a licensed, certified backflow tester. This confirms the device is working correctly and continues to protect your potable water supply from contamination. We can also help with scheduling these annual tests.

    We regularly install high-quality backflow preventers from trusted brands such as Watts, Wilkins by Zurn, Apollo and Conbraco. Our plumbers are experienced with a wide range of devices and can recommend the best option for your application and budget.

    Installing a backflow preventer is a complex task that requires plumbing knowledge, tools and certification to meet local codes and properly protect your water supply. Incorrect installation can lead to ineffective protection, fines or health risks. It is always recommended to hire a licensed professional like CKV Plumbing.

    An RPZ, or reduced pressure zone device, provides the highest level of protection and is used for high-hazard applications where contaminants could be toxic.
