    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Burlington

    A standard flush and inspection typically runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement usually adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend yearly hot water tank maintenance. This is especially true where water is hard, since minerals speed up sediment buildup and anode rod depletion.

    The anode rod, also known as a sacrificial rod, is a metal rod inside your hot water tank designed to corrode before the tank itself. It protects the tank lining from rust and corrosion, which matters most when water carries a lot of minerals, and it extends the lifespan of your unit.

    That sound is water trapped under a layer of sediment boiling as the burner fires. A flush usually quiets it, though very old hardened scale may not come out completely.

    Common signs include reduced hot water supply, odd noises like rumbling or popping, rust-colored water, a sulfur or rotten egg smell, or water leaking from the tank. These point to issues that maintenance can often resolve.

    Once a year is the baseline for most homes, and more often where water is hard. Homes without a softener or with heavy use may do better with a flush every six months.
