    Pump and Pressure Tank Matching in Burlington

    Pump and tank work as a pair. A tank that is too small or has lost its air charge makes the pump start dozens of extra times a day, and every start heats the motor. Every pump job includes a tank check.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Burlington, VT

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. Lost prime is the classic complaint.
