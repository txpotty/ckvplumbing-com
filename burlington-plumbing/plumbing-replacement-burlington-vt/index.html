    Homes in Burlington, VT with older galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes often experience low water pressure, discolored water or frequent leaks.     Water Line Replacement in Burlington, VT

    A leaking main water line can lead to significant water loss, high bills and foundation damage, which is a serious concern for homes on slab foundations. Our water line replacement services address issues like corroded pipes, leaks or low water pressure. We make sure of a secure, leak-free connection from the meter to your home, restoring reliable water flow.

    Water Softener Replacement in Burlington, VT

    Hard water leads to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. Some Vermont wells carry iron, manganese or other naturally occurring minerals, so a water test is the first step before choosing treatment. If your old water softener is no longer effectively removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, sized for your household's water usage and installed correctly on your main water line to provide truly soft water throughout your Burlington home.

    If you have isolated leaks on newer pipes, a repair might suffice.
