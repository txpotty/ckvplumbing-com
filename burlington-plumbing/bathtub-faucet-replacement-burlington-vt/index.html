    Frequently Asked Questions About Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Burlington

    Most tub faucet replacements run $250 to $600 with a standard faucet. Trim and cartridge work costs the least, and full valve replacement behind tile costs the most. You get a written estimate first.

    One to four hours for most jobs. A spout or a cartridge swap is often done in an hour, while a full valve replacement that needs an access panel or a tile opening, soldering and a pressure test can take most of a morning. You hear the expected time before we begin.

    Plan on three to five hours. We open the wall, swap the valve, pressure-test it and then install the trim and cover. Any tile patch takes longer.

    Trim and cartridge work usually does not need one. In-wall valve replacement can need a permit, especially if supply pipes change. We pull it when required.
