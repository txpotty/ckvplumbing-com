    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Burlington

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Emergency Leak Detection in Burlington, VT

    Water coming through a ceiling at night cannot wait until morning. Shut the main. We respond to Burlington, VT emergencies with full detection gear, isolate the failed line and restore water to the rest of the house while the permanent repair gets scheduled.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Burlington, VT

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. A green stripe in a dry yard is a hint. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, and on plastic pipe we inject tracer gas and sniff for it at the surface.

    Pool Leak Detection in Burlington

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain so we can point to the exact fitting or crack.

    Gas Leak Detection in Burlington

    A faint rotten-egg odor by the furnace or stove needs a professional check. Leave the house if the odor is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, with repairs made to the fuel gas code before the gas comes back on.

    Water Line Leak Detection in Burlington, VT

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. Your water bill is usually the first warning. We read the meter, close fixture stops one at a time and check framing with a moisture meter, then confirm with acoustic gear on pressurized lines, which keeps the drywall cut small in your Burlington home.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in Burlington

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, which makes it tricky to separate evaporation from a real leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, because a running leak can undermine the soil around the pool.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in Burlington, VT

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. We handle both every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.
