    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in Burlington, VT

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A typical 40-gallon gas tank uses around 40,000 BTU, while a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the gas line from the meter almost always gets upsized. Venting changes too. Both are on the estimate.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Burlington

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in Burlington

    Sizing a home unit depends on two figures: peak gallons per minute and temperature rise. A shower head flows about 2 gallons per minute and a kitchen faucet a bit less, so a house that runs two showers and a sink together needs roughly 5 to 6 gallons at the rise your inlet water calls for. That narrows the model list fast.

    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Burlington, VT

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Town water systems in Burlington and other communities are generally reliable, but older service lines can add sediment and discolored water. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. Flush it yearly. Service valves make it easy.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Burlington, VT

    Businesses in Burlington, VT with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank. One unit can fail and the rest still carry the load. We size the rack by peak demand. Commercial installs usually need a larger gas service and a mechanical permit.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Burlington

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job since the tank's location may not suit the new unit. Mounting near the gas meter on a garage or outside wall shortens the gas line and venting, though it can add distance to the bathrooms. We weigh both. A far bath may need recirc.

    Tankless vs Tank Cost Comparison in Burlington, VT

    Upfront, a tankless install in Burlington, VT costs more than a standard tank because of the unit price, the gas line upgrade and the venting changes. Over the long haul, the math narrows. Lower standby loss, a lifespan of up to 20 years against about 10 for a tank, and parts that can be replaced instead of the whole unit all count toward the total cost of ownership. Ask for both prices.
