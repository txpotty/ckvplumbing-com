    Pressure Tank Repair in Burlington, VT

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. Fast cycling kills pumps young. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Burlington

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. These pumps can also leak at the shaft seal or wear out an impeller. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Burlington

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. We replace the switch, clean or replace the tube and set the cut-in and cut-out pressures to match your tank, typically 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Constant Pressure System Repair in Burlington, VT

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction. The drive, sensor and wiring get tested, and the failed part gets repaired or replaced before we verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Burlington

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. CKV Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Burlington, VT well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Emergency Well Pump Repair in Burlington

    No water at all means a household on hold. CKV Plumbing handles emergency well pump calls in Burlington at the top of the schedule. Technicians carry gauges, an amp clamp and the usual switches and capacitors, so many no-water calls are fixed on the first trip. A full pump swap takes longer.

    Well Pump Diagnostic Services in Burlington, VT

    Voltage, amperage, pressure and flow rate tell us most of what is happening down the well without pulling a single length of pipe. A weak motor draws high amps. Low flow with normal amp draw points to a plugged intake or a falling water level, which is a different repair. Testing first saves a pull.

    Low Water Pressure Diagnosis in Burlington

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. Common causes include a plugged sediment filter, a low switch setting or a yard line leak. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.
