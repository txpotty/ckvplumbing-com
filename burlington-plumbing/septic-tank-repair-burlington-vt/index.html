    Septic Line Clog Clearing in Burlington

    Drain Field Repair in Burlington, VT

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload caused by a leaking fixture are the other usual causes. We test before recommending anything big. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Septic Tee Repair in Burlington

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer flows straight into the field lines and seals the trenches. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Burlington

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. We replace failing lids and add risers that bring access up to the lawn. Future pumping gets cheaper. Every lid we set is screwed down or locked so a child cannot lift it.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Burlington

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. Our tech confirms the pump runs and looks for a float snagged on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.

    Septic Tank Leak Repair in Burlington

    A tank that never fills to the outlet, or one that fills slowly with groundwater after rain, has a leak. We pump it down and look at the walls and seams with a light, and dye flushed down a fixture shows us where the water moves. Hairline cracks get a hydraulic cement patch. Split tanks get replaced.

    House-to-Tank Line Repair in Burlington
