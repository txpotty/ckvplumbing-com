    Main Line Hydro Jetting in Burlington

    A jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, not just a hole through the middle. That matters a lot.     Hydro-Jetting for Stubborn Clogs in Burlington

    Some lines get cabled three times a year and still clog. The cable is not the fix. Those lines are usually coated in grease or scale, and a cable just cuts a small hole that closes back up. Jetting removes the coating down to the pipe wall. It lasts much longer.

    Residential Clogged Sewer Lines in Burlington

    Most home sewer clogs come from three things: roots, grease and wipes. Often all three together. Grease coats the pipe, wipes catch on it and roots lock everything into a plug. We clear it with a sewer machine, then camera the line to see which one started it.

Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Toss wipes in the trash.
