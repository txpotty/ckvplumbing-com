    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Burlington

    A standard single tank softener replacement typically costs $1,500 to $3,000 installed. Dual tank or premium systems usually run $3,000 to $5,000. Adding a new loop or drain line can raise the cost.

    New water softeners typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years on different components, such as the control valve and resin tank. CKV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work for your peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be provided with your quote.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and proven performance.

    Most softener replacements take two to four hours. New loops or drain lines can take a bit longer.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the appropriate softening capacity in grains per day for your Burlington home, so the system keeps up.

    We test your hardness in grains per gallon and estimate daily water usage. That tells us the resin capacity needed.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had heavy mineral buildup restricting flow. However, a water softener's main job is to remove hardness minerals, not to boost pressure. If low water pressure is your main concern, we can investigate other causes.

    Most softeners last 10 to 15 years. Resin and valves wear faster with very hard or chlorinated water.
