    Expansion Tank Installation in Burlington, VT

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and plumbing.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Burlington, VT

    Electric water heaters are common in homes without natural gas access or where homeowners prefer electric appliances. Our installation process for electric units in Burlington includes verifying correct electrical wiring, breaker sizing and secure connections. We ensure that the new unit meets all electrical safety standards and local building codes. While electric units can have higher operating costs than gas in some areas, they are often simpler to install and maintain. We'll help you select an efficient model that fits your budget and needs.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Burlington

    Many homes in Burlington use natural gas for their water heaters due to its efficiency and low operating costs. When replacing a gas water heater, precise gas line connections, proper ventilation and carbon monoxide detection are critical. Our licensed plumbers are experts in gas appliance installation, ensuring every safety measure is met. We check for leaks, test the igniter and confirm proper exhaust, adhering to all local Vermont codes. You can trust us to safely and effectively install your new gas water heater, providing dependable hot water.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Burlington

    Some local homes have water heaters in attics or interior closets. These installs require careful planning for access, drain pans and relief line routing. Attic temperatures can swing widely with the seasons, so we take extra steps to protect connections. We install drain pans with proper drain lines to protect ceilings. If relocation makes sense, we can move the heater to a better spot.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Burlington, VT

    Tankless water heaters provide on-demand hot water, saving energy and space, and they are more and more popular in Vermont homes. Our experts specialize in installing these efficient units, ensuring proper gas line sizing or electrical service upgrades if needed. We also address venting requirements, which are often different from traditional units. While the upfront cost can be higher, the long-term energy savings and endless hot water supply make them an attractive option for many homeowners in Burlington. We'll help you choose the right model for your specific needs.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Burlington

    Gas tank water heaters provide strong recovery and are common in many local homes. We install 40, 50 and 75 gallon models with proper venting and combustion air. Each install includes a new gas connector, sediment trap and shutoff valve. We check draft and test for gas leaks. Gas units are a good choice for families with high hot water demand.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Installation in Burlington

    The cost of water heater installation in Burlington, VT usually ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of water heater (tank vs. tankless), its capacity, fuel type (gas or electric) and any needed plumbing or electrical modifications. We provide a written, upfront quote after assessing your particular needs.

    A standard tank replacement typically takes two to four hours. Tankless installs or relocations can take most of a day.

    Rusty water, leaks around the base, rumbling noises and running out of hot water quickly are common signs. If your heater is over 10 years old, replacement is often wise.

    The decision to repair or replace often depends on the age of your unit, the cost of the repair and its energy efficiency. If your water heater is over 10 years old, often breaks down, or the repair cost is more than 50% of a new unit, replacement is often the more economical choice. Newer models also offer significantly better energy efficiency.

    If your home has a pressure reducing valve or check valve, an expansion tank is typically required. It protects your water heater and pipes from pressure spikes.

    Most households of two to four people do well with a 40 or 50 gallon tank. Larger families may need a 75 gallon tank or a tankless unit sized by flow rate.

    New water heaters usually come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and components, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. CKV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation work, giving you peace of mind. We will discuss exact warranty details for your chosen unit during the consultation.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. For tank units, a 40 to 50 gallon tank is common for 2 to 4 people, while larger families might need 60 to 80 gallons. For tankless units, it is about flow rate (GPM) and temperature rise. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.
