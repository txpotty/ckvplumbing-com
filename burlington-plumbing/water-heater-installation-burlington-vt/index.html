    Expansion Tank Installation in Burlington, VT

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. The tank soaks up that extra volume. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Burlington, VT

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Burlington

    Swapping a gas water heater comes down to three connections done right, the gas, the vent and the water. We install a new flex connector with a sediment trap, check vent pitch and draft and test every joint with a gas leak detector. Carbon monoxide is the risk. We follow code on all of it.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Burlington

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. Ceilings are at stake. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Burlington, VT

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Gas Tank Water Heater Installation in Burlington

    A gas tank is the most common water heater we install, in 40, 50 and 75 gallon sizes. Each one gets a new gas connector, a sediment trap and a shutoff valve, plus a vent connector pitched up toward the flue at a quarter inch per foot. We test draft before leaving. Gas suits busy households.

    Water Heater Installation Cost in Burlington, VT

    Up front, a tank heater costs less than tankless. You also get an estimate of yearly energy cost for each option.
