    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Burlington, VT

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Renovations in Burlington frequently mix original plumbing with newer copper or PEX, so a thorough look at transitions and fittings is important. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Aggressive cutters can break through thin spots, so we use descaling chains and careful jetting made for older iron, which restores flow and gives a clear view of the remaining pipe wall on camera.

    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Burlington, VT

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. Every drain shares that one pipe. After the line is open, a camera run tells us if the blockage was debris or something structural that will keep catching waste.

    Root Masses in Burlington Sewer Laterals

    Mature trees and old clay laterals do not mix. Roots find the joints. We cut roots in several passes, then look on camera to judge the damage and talk about lining or a spot repair.

    Grease and Scale Buildup Cleaning in Burlington, VT

    Grease bonds with mineral scale into a hard crust. A large share of Vermont homes rely on private wells, and well water here can range from soft to moderately hard depending on the bedrock. Flow slows a little each year. A cable can punch a hole through the crust, but a jetter cleans the walls back to full size and leaves less for new grease to grab onto, and the method depends on pipe material and condition.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Burlington

    Household sewers clog slowly. Grease, hair and wipes build up over months.     Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Burlington, VT

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. A crack stays a crack. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.
