    Electric Tank Repair in Burlington, VT

    Four parts can fail on an electric tank, two elements and two thermostats. Scale from hard water buries the lower element and burns it out. After testing resistance and voltage, we replace what failed, reset the high limit and watch it heat up. A breaker that keeps tripping gets traced first.

    Tankless Water Heater Repair in Burlington

    Scale is the main enemy of a tankless heater. It coats the heat exchanger and trips the unit into an error code. A descaling flush, a clean inlet screen and a check of the igniter and flow sensor cover most tankless calls. All major brands.

    Tankless Unit Repair in Burlington

    Navien, Rinnai, Noritz and Rheem tankless units show error codes on the front panel that help us narrow the problem quickly. Common causes are scale on the heat exchanger, a dirty inlet filter, a failed flow sensor or a weak igniter. We descale with a pump and approved solution.
