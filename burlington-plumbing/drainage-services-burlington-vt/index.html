    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Burlington

    Drain clearing in Burlington usually runs $125 to $500 depending on the line, and hydro jetting runs $350 to $800. French drains cost more. Most residential French drain or downspout projects land between $2,000 and $8,000, depending on length, depth and how much hardscape we have to cut through or work around.

    A typical French drain or downspout system takes one to three days. Larger projects run longer. Dry wells, long trenches or saw-cut concrete can push it toward a week.

    They solve different problems. French drains handle water moving through soil. Channel drains catch surface water on concrete, and plenty of yards need both.

    Call when more than one drain is slow, or when a toilet gurgles while the washer drains. That usually means the main line. Outside, call when water stands in the yard for more than a day after rain, when a downspout overflows the gutter in every storm, or when water shows up in the crawlspace or basement after a heavy rain.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. Clean them when they slow down. Homes with root-prone sewers, old cast iron, or a kitchen that sends a lot of grease down the drain do better with a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the line slows to a crawl.

    Store-bought cleaners rarely clear a real clog. They often make it worse. They sit in the trap, can damage pipe and joints, and leave caustic liquid for the plumber. A cable is safer.

    Most sink and toilet clogs clear in under an hour. Main lines take longer. A main line with camera work usually takes about two hours, more if the cleanout is hard to reach.

    Installations like French drains, dry wells and sewer repairs carry a workmanship warranty. Clearing has its own guarantee. If a drain we installed does not perform as promised, we fix it at no labor cost.
