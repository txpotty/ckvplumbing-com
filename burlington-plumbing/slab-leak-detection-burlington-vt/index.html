    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Burlington, VT

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Burlington.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Burlington

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which specific line is compromised. This systematic approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, giving you an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Burlington, VT.

    Slab Leak Detection and Repair Cost Comparison in Burlington, VT

    Slab leak detection usually costs $150 to $450. Direct access repairs usually run $1,000 to $3,000, including concrete patching but not flooring replacement. Reroutes generally cost $800 to $3,500 depending on distance and access. Full repipes cost more, often $4,000 to $15,000, but end the problem for good.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Burlington

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the slab and runs new pipe through the walls or attic. It avoids breaking concrete and often costs less than direct access under finished floors. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it handles heat and resists hard-water corrosion. This option is popular when the slab is covered in tile or hardwood.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Burlington, VT

    Sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages or separations. This is important if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Burlington home.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Burlington

    For elusive leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This technology allows us to locate leaks in various pipe materials, including copper, PEX and PVC, commonly found in Burlington homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.
