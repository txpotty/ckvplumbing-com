    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Burlington, VT

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long.     Commercial Faucet Repair in Burlington, VT

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house.     Ball Faucet Repair in Burlington, VT

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Burlington, VT

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. Each brand uses its own part number. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Burlington, VT

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. Cracked or discontinued faucets are different. You get a repair price and a replacement price so the choice is yours.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Burlington

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. It happens faster with hard water. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Burlington

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Burlington

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. We figure out which type you have first. It matters. With the right part on the truck, most drips are fixed in one visit.
