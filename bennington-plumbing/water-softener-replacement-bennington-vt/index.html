    Picking the Right Softener for Bennington, VT Homes

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Bennington, VT

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. Outdoor hose bibs usually stay hard. Why soften water for the lawn?

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Bennington

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Installed to spec, the warranty stays valid.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Bennington, VT

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Discontinued valves usually mean replacement.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Bennington

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Bennington

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Sometimes the whole unit goes.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Bennington

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. Metered valves like the Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 count gallons and regenerate only when the resin is close to spent. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Bennington, VT

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. We can usually replace a leaking or stuck system within a day or two, and same day if we have the size on the truck.

    Softener Loop Installation in Bennington, VT

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.
