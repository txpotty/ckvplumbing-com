    Resin Tank and Media Replacement in Bennington, VT

    Softener resin can break down after 10 to 15 years, especially with chlorinated city water. When resin fails, you will see hard water symptoms even though the system is regenerating. Sometimes replacing resin and the tank is more cost effective than a whole new system. We inspect the valve and brine tank to decide. If the valve is old, a full replacement often makes better sense.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Bennington

    Older timer-based softeners regenerate on a set schedule whether you used water or not. Metered softeners track actual usage and regenerate only when the resin is nearly used up. This saves salt, water and wear on the system. We replace old timer valves with metered Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1 valves. Most homeowners notice lower salt use within the first few weeks.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Replacement in Bennington

    The cost of water softener replacement in Bennington, VT can vary widely, typically ranging from $1,500 to $4,000. This depends on the type and size of the new system, labor costs and any needed plumbing changes. We provide transparent, upfront pricing after assessing your needs.

    A large share of Vermont homes rely on private wells, and well water here can range from soft to fairly hard depending on the bedrock. Hard water contains dissolved minerals like calcium and magnesium. These minerals can lead to scale buildup in pipes, lower efficiency of water heaters and appliances, spots on dishes, and dry skin and hair. A working water softener is the best defense for combating these issues in Bennington homes.

    We install systems with Fleck, Clack and Kinetico valves, among others. These brands have good parts availability and reliable performance.

    The right size depends on your household's daily water usage and the hardness of your water in grains per gallon. Our technicians will perform a water test and calculate the appropriate softening capacity in grains per day for your Bennington home, so the system performs well.

    New water softeners usually come with two types of warranties. We stand behind our work with a solid warranty for your peace of mind.

    A new, properly sized water softener can sometimes improve water pressure if your old unit was undersized or had significant mineral buildup restricting flow.     In many areas, a plumbing permit is not usually required for a like-for-like water softener replacement, especially if there are no significant changes to the existing plumbing lines.
