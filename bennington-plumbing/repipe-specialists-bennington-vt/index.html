    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Bennington

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.

    Emergency Pipe Failures and Repipes in Bennington

    Sometimes a house reaches the repipe point all at once, with a burst line and two more leaks right behind it.     Galvanized Pipe Removal in Bennington

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. New PEX or copper stops the rust, restores pressure and ends leaks at the threads, which is where galvanized fails first. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Bennington

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Rocky glacial soil and ledge are common across Vermont, which can make digging for water lines, sewer repairs or septic work slow and costly. Rather than jackhammer to reach one leak, we run new lines through the attic and walls and leave the old under-slab pipe out of service. No jackhammer needed. It ends this leak and takes the rest of the slab plumbing out of the picture.

    Drain Line Repiping in Bennington

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Bennington

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. We sweat joints with lead-free solder or press them, hang the pipe at proper support intervals and test the whole system before patching.

    Urgent Repipes After Repeated Failures in Bennington, VT

    A widespread pipe failure sometimes forces a repipe sooner than you planned. The first step is temporary repairs so you have water, and then we book the repipe as quickly as the permit and schedule allow. We move fast. Even urgent repipes usually take just two to four days after we begin.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Bennington

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Repipe Specialists in Bennington

    Most PEX repipes for Vermont homes cost $5,000 to $12,000, based on size, bathroom count and access. Copper repipes usually run 20 to 40 percent more.

    Both work well, and the best choice depends on your house and your water. PEX is cheaper. PEX is flexible, handles a freeze better and resists scale, while copper is rigid, tolerates sunlight and has a long history, but it costs more and can pinhole in aggressive water.
