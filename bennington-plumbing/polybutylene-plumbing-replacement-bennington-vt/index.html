    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Bennington

    Most PEX replacements of polybutylene cost $5,000 to $12,000 for an average home. Copper costs more, and yard service lines add $1,500 to $4,000.

    We primarily use quality, trusted brands for PEX such as Uponor (formerly Wirsbo), Viega, and Zurn, known for their durability and performance. For copper, we use standard Type L or Type M copper tubing from reputable manufacturers, providing long-lasting reliability.

    Both PEX and copper are excellent choices. PEX is more flexible, resistant to freezing, and generally more affordable for installation. Copper is highly durable, lasts a very long time, and is resistant to UV light. We'll help you choose the best option for your Vermont home and budget.

    We provide a written workmanship warranty on the repipe, and pipe manufacturers offer their own long-term warranties. We explain coverage before work begins.
