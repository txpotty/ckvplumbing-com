    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Bennington, VT

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. We test them first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Bennington

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Replacement control boxes are easy to source. We match the motor to the pump end and the wire size to the run length.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Bennington, VT

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They are pricier than a standard pump. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    Pulling a Submersible Pump in Bennington

    Pulling a submersible means lifting the pump and every length of drop pipe out through the pitless adapter, sometimes hundreds of feet of it. We check the torque arrestor and every splice on the way up. Most jobs are done in a day.

    Well Pressure Tank Replacement in Bennington

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes a healthy pump look sick. If the pump clicks on every time someone runs a faucet for ten seconds, the bladder in the tank has probably failed and the pump is short cycling itself to death. A tire gauge on the air valve tells us a lot. Precharge should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure.

    Agricultural and Ranch Well Pumps in Bennington

    Irrigation and stock wells often run 3 to 10 HP pumps, sometimes on three-phase power with a motor starter and overload protection at the panel. Starters and overloads get replaced along with the pump. We schedule around watering times.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Bennington

    Sand kills submersibles early. Grit in a well chews up impellers fast, so we look for it on the old pump and may set the new one higher or add a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Bennington, VT

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. A two-pipe deep-well jet can reach further down at the cost of efficiency. The common failure is losing prime. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.
