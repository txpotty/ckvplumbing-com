    Pedestal Sink Installation in Bennington, VT

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. The wall needs solid blocking. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Bennington, VT

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in Bennington, VT

    Labor is the main cost driver. Moving the drain or adding a second bowl adds hours, and a straight drop-in swap is the cheapest. The sink price is separate. You can buy it or we can.

    Faucet and Drain Assembly for New Sinks in Bennington, VT

    Faucet and drain go on before the sink is set, when possible. Setting the faucet and drain while the bowl is on sawhorses is faster and seals better than working in the cabinet. Undermounts are the exception. Those get plumbed from below.

    Pedestal Sink Mounting in Bennington

    The pedestal is mostly for looks. The basin itself hangs on a bracket lagged into 2x blocking behind the drywall, and if the old wall has no blocking we open it, add a board between the studs, and patch it back. Anchors in drywall will fail. A loose basin cracks the trap.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Bennington

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. We check the vent size too. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Bennington, VT

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. Paper towels catch the smallest drip. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Bennington

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. The bowl hangs on clips with a full bead of silicone, and it stays braced overnight while the adhesive cures. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.
