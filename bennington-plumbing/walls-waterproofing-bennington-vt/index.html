    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in Bennington

    Older homes often still have galvanized supply lines, cast iron drains or even lead service lines that deserve inspection and replacement.     Shower Wall Waterproofing in Bennington

    Tile and grout are not waterproof on their own, so water reaches the backer board in every shower. If the walls were built without a proper membrane, moisture soaks into studs and drywall. We remove failing tile, install cement board or foam board and apply a membrane such as Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban. Seams, corners and niches get extra attention before new tile is set.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Bennington

    Many homes in Bennington have crawl spaces, which are very prone to moisture issues. Installing a heavy-duty vapor barrier on crawl space walls and floors is a key waterproofing strategy. This thick polyethylene sheeting prevents ground moisture from evaporating into the crawl space, protecting subflooring and joists and improving indoor air quality. It's an effective way to control humidity and prevent wood rot.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Bennington, VT

    Interior walls waterproofing focuses on managing water that has already penetrated the foundation walls. This usually involves installing an interior drainage system, often called a French drain, along the perimeter of the basement floor. Water is collected and channeled to a sump pump, which then sends it safely away from the foundation. While not preventing water from entering the wall, it effectively stops it from pooling inside your living space. This solution is less disruptive than exterior excavation and is suitable where exterior access is limited.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Bennington

    Crawl space encapsulation involves sealing the crawl space entirely with heavy-duty vapor barriers and often includes a dehumidifier. This not only waterproofs the walls but also controls humidity, preventing mold growth and wood rot in the crawl space. A long, cold heating season is the defining stress on Vermont plumbing, with months of freezing nights and repeated freeze and thaw cycles. An encapsulated crawl space can significantly improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency by keeping moisture from moving up into the home. We use tough, reinforced poly sheeting for walls and floors, sealed at all seams and penetrations.

    Drainage System Integration in Bennington, VT

    Effective walls waterproofing often requires a robust drainage system to divert water away from your foundation. This can include interior perimeter drains that channel water to a sump pump, or exterior French drains that collect surface water.     Frequently Asked Questions About Walls Waterproofing in Bennington

    Shower wall waterproofing with a membrane system usually runs $1,200 to $3,500 including demolition and backer board, not counting new tile. Exterior block wall sealing often costs $800 to $3,000 depending on size. Below-grade foundation waterproofing usually ranges from $2,500 to $8,000.

    Walls waterproofing is generally not recommended as a DIY project due to its complexity and the high importance of a sound foundation. It often involves heavy excavation, specialized equipment, precise material application, and knowledge of drainage principles and local building codes. Improper waterproofing can lead to more severe and costly damage down the line, so professional help is highly advisable.

    Walls waterproofing is a primary step in preventing future mold growth by eliminating the moisture source. However, it does not remove existing mold. If mold is already present, it will need to be professionally remediated before or along with the waterproofing process. Once the area is dry and mold-free, waterproofing will help keep it that way.

    We use a range of high-performance waterproofing membranes and coatings depending on the application and foundation type. These can include liquid asphalt-modified polyurethane, rubberized asphalt membranes, bentonite clay systems, and crystalline coatings. Brands often include well-known names known for their durability and effectiveness in preventing water penetration.

    For extensive walls waterproofing projects that involve excavation, significant structural changes, or changes to drainage systems, a permit from the Bennington building department may be required. We will advise you on any necessary permits and help with the application process so all work complies with local codes.

    Interior waterproofing addresses water after it has entered the foundation, usually managing it with sealants and drainage systems inside the basement or crawl space. Exterior waterproofing prevents water from reaching the foundation walls in the first place, usually by excavating and applying membranes or coatings to the outside of the foundation.

    Damp proofing is a less robust solution, typically a spray-on asphalt coating that resists soil moisture but isn't designed to withstand hydrostatic pressure. Waterproofing uses stronger membranes, coatings, or drainage systems specifically engineered to prevent liquid water from entering, even under pressure. For basements or crawl spaces in areas with high water tables or heavy rainfall, waterproofing is the right choice.
