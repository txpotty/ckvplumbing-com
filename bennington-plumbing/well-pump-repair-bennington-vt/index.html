    Jet Pump System Repair in Bennington, VT

    Jet pumps, usually found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Typical problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. CKV Plumbing can reliably troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Bennington operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is essential for jet pump function.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Bennington

    Submersible pumps are common in residential wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms usually include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance.
