    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Bennington, VT: The Details

    Undermount Kitchen Sink Installation in Bennington

    Undermount sinks rely on clips and adhesive to stay attached to granite or quartz. We mount the bowl, seal the rim with silicone and brace it while the adhesive sets, which usually takes overnight before the sink can hold a full load of water. Strainers and traps go in after.

    Commercial Kitchen Sink Installation in Bennington, VT

    Three-compartment sinks, prep sinks and hand sinks carry separate health code requirements. Prep sinks usually need an indirect drain to a floor sink with an air gap, while the three-compartment sink often runs through the grease trap. The local rule gets checked first. Work is scheduled before or after service so the kitchen does not close.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Bennington

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Bennington, VT

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. A support frame goes into the cabinet and the sink is leveled before the countertop goes on. A deep bowl means a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Same-Day Sink Replacement in Bennington, VT

    A sink with a crack through the bowl or a strainer that has rusted out cannot be patched for long. We stock the most common double-bowl stainless sizes, so a same-day swap is often possible, and we replace the strainers and trap at the same time. Old stops come out too.

    Restaurant Sink Installation in Bennington

    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Bennington

    Composite sinks outweigh stainless bowls and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. We check the strainer fit first. If the included strainer is too short for the sink wall, we swap in one that fits rather than overtightening and risking a chip around the opening.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Bennington

    Both bowls of a double sink share one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.
