    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Bennington

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate.     Heat Exchanger Descaling in Bennington

    Picture a bundle of thin tubes with a gas flame under them and cold water racing through at three or four gallons a minute. That is your heat exchanger. Calcium and magnesium drop out on the hottest tube walls first and narrow the passages. We dissolve it with 45 to 60 minutes of pump-driven descaling solution.

    Condensate and Neutralizer Service in Bennington

    Condensing tankless heaters drip acidic condensate, often around pH 3 to 4, and it needs to pass through a neutralizer before it reaches a drain. We clean the trap and look at the neutralizer media. Spent media gets replaced. Where the line runs outside, we check that it is supported and protected.

    Tankless Error Code Diagnosis in Bennington, VT

    An error code is the heater telling you what it saw. Rinnai LC codes point to scale, Navien E003 is a failed ignition and Noritz 90 points at combustion air or exhaust trouble. First we read the code history. If a part has failed, you hear the repair and the price before we start.
