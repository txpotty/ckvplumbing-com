    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Bennington, VT

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Bennington, VT

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Rebates may apply. We will tell you if your space works.

    Commercial Water Heater Installation in Bennington

    Restaurants, salons and offices burn through hot water at rates no household tank can keep up with. Our commercial work covers high-BTU tanks and multi-unit tankless racks, along with the larger gas lines, venting and code inspections that come with them. Downtime costs you money. Night installs are available.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Bennington

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. Every attic heater needs a drain pan with a line to the outside or a drain, a relief line routed properly and a clear path for the next tank to come in and out. A leak lands on drywall. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.

    Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Bennington, VT

    Water on the garage floor and no hot water usually means the tank has rusted through. First step is to close the cold water valve above the tank and shut off the gas or the breaker, which stops the leak from getting worse. Common tanks ride on our trucks. Same-day replacement is normal.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Bennington

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. New installations, water heater replacements and significant repairs typically need a permit, especially in multi-unit and rental buildings. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We set the air charge to match your water pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Electric Water Heater Installation in Bennington, VT

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. They do not need a vent. That makes placement easier.
