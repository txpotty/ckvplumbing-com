    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Bennington, VT

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Emergency Sump Pump Replacement in Bennington, VT

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Bennington, VT

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Replacement switches are easy to get. A small weep hole in the discharge pipe keeps the pump from air locking.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Bennington

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause. The outlet sits well clear of the foundation and the property line.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Bennington, VT

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Bennington

    No pit yet? We saw-cut the slab at the low corner, break out the concrete and dig down through soil, hardpan or rock to set an 18 by 22 inch basin. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. The slab gets patched with new concrete. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Bennington

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Bennington

    Sump pump installation in Bennington, VT runs about $800 to $2,500 for most homes. The biggest swing comes from whether we are swapping a pump in an existing pit or cutting a new pit through the slab, which adds concrete work and several hours of labor. Backups add to it. You get a written price after we look at the space.

    A submersible sits in the pit under water, so it runs quieter and takes no floor space. The pedestal type mounts the motor on a column above the water, which makes it easier to reach for service and usually cheaper to buy, though you will hear it. Either can do the job. We help you pick based on your basement.

    Submersibles are quieter and stronger, so they suit heavy use. Pedestal pumps cost less and are easy to service, though they are louder.

    Zoeller, Little Giant, Wayne and Liberty make up most of what we install. Each of those brands has parts available locally, which matters when a switch fails on a Saturday and you need it fixed that weekend. The model depends on your pit and lift.

    Grinding, rattling or a pump that runs with no water in the pit are all warning signs. Visible rust, a float that sticks or a pump that cycles every minute during dry weather usually means the end is near. If the basement flooded anyway, it is time.

    Most last seven to ten years. Pumps that sit idle for long stretches can fail sooner, so test yours a few times a year.

    Odd noises, rust, constant cycling or a pump that fails to start are the common signs. Age matters too. An inspection settles it.
