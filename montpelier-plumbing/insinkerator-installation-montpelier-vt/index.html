    Hard Water and Mineral Considerations in Montpelier

    Some Vermont wells carry iron, manganese or other naturally occurring minerals, so a lab test is the first step before choosing treatment. Hard water leaves scale on disposal grind rings, sink flanges and drain tailpieces. Over time that buildup can make a disposal noisy and cause slow drainage. We inspect the drain line for scale and debris before connecting a new unit. If heavy buildup is present, we recommend cleaning or replacing the trap and tailpiece at the same time. Stainless steel grind components on Evolution models resist corrosion better in these conditions.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Montpelier

    Most InSinkErator units have a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. We knock that plug out, clear the debris and connect the dishwasher drain hose with a proper clamp. We then install or verify an air gap or high loop so dirty sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher. Forgetting the knockout is one of the most common DIY mistakes and leads to a dishwasher that will not drain. We test both appliances together before we leave.
