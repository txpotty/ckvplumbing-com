    No hot water at all is usually a pilot, a gas valve, a tripped high-limit or a dead element. Those are fixable.     Expansion Tank Service in Montpelier, VT

    A thermal expansion tank is the small steel tank on the cold line above the heater, and it gives heated water somewhere to go when a check valve or pressure reducer closes the system.     Burner and Pilot Service in Montpelier, VT

    On a gas tank, a dirty burner throws a lazy yellow flame and leaves soot on the flue baffle. The burner comes out and gets brushed and vacuumed.     Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Montpelier, VT

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. On gas units we inspect the burner, thermocouple and vent connector. Electric units get a meter test on both elements and a look at the wiring at each thermostat, where loose terminals can scorch and melt the insulation. Both get a flush.

    Standard Tank Tune-Up in Montpelier

    A standard tune-up starts with the fuel off and the cold supply closed, then a full drain through the bottom valve until the water runs clear. Figure on half an hour or a bit more for the drain.
