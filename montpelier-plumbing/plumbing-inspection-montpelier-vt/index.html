    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Montpelier

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. We test the T and P valve, confirm its discharge line runs to a safe place, look for an expansion tank where one is needed and check the drain pan. Gas units get a CO check.     Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Montpelier, VT

    Short summers with occasional humidity bring condensation on cold pipes in basements, which can lead to drips and mold if left alone. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. Outdoor faucets, exposed pipe, irrigation backflow devices and the heater drain and relief valve all get checked. We look at pipe insulation. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.
