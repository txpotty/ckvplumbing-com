    PEX Pipe Installation in Montpelier

    PEX costs less than copper and goes in faster. Scale and rust do not affect it.     Whole-Home Re-Piping in Montpelier

    A whole-home repipe replaces every supply line from the main shutoff to each fixture, including the water heater connections. Most single-story homes take two to three days. We run new trunk lines through the attic or crawlspace, branch down to fixtures through small wall openings, with service restored each evening when possible. Then patching.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Montpelier, VT

    Pinhole leaks start small. First a damp spot, then drywall damage. They usually mean the copper wall has thinned from corrosion, and when one shows up, more are often on the way along the same lines. Repipe or patch, we explain.

    Slab Leak Pipe Replacement in Montpelier

    Rerouting is often better than digging. Breaking a slab is messy and costly. We run new pipe through the attic or walls to bypass the leaking line under the concrete, and usually costs less than repairing in place. Fast too.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Montpelier, VT

    Copper still has fans. It handles heat well and lasts decades. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, while strapping each line properly, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.
