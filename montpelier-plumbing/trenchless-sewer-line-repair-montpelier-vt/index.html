    Sewer Camera Inspections for Trenchless Repair in Montpelier

    Before any trenchless repair, a detailed sewer camera inspection is crucial. Our high-definition cameras travel through your sewer lines, providing real-time video of the pipe's interior. This allows us to precisely diagnose the problem, identify its exact location and determine the most appropriate trenchless method, whether it is pipe bursting or CIPP.     Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Montpelier

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints. Pipe bursting is often the best option for Orangeburg, and lining can work well for clay that still holds its shape. We determine the material on camera before recommending a method.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Montpelier

    Cured-in-place lining creates a new pipe inside the old one. We clean the line, then pull or invert a resin-saturated liner into place and cure it with hot water, steam or UV light. The result is a seamless, jointless pipe that roots cannot penetrate. Lining works well for cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their form.

    Sectional Point Repair in Montpelier

    When only one section of a line is damaged, a point repair can fix it without lining the whole length. We position a short resin liner at the damaged spot using the camera and inflate it until it cures. Point repairs are a cost-effective fix for a single crack, hole or root entry. They are a good fit on otherwise healthy lines.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Montpelier, VT

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable and watertight liner. This technique is well suited for repairing cracks, leaks and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It is a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Vermont homeowners.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Montpelier, VT

    Cast iron drains and sewer lines corrode from the inside, leaving rough scale, cracks and channel rot along the bottom of the pipe. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or cutters, then line it to restore a smooth, sealed interior. This is especially useful for cast iron running under slabs where excavation is costly. Lining can add many years of service.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Montpelier

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one while fracturing the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene pipe, which is very resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in older Montpelier homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.
