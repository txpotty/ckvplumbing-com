    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Montpelier

    Kitec, with its orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe and brass fittings, and gray polybutylene pipe are both known for failures.     Copper Repiping in Montpelier, VT

    Some homeowners prefer copper for its long track record and rigidity. We install Type L copper, which has a thicker wall than the Type M used in many older homes. Joints are soldered with lead-free solder or pressed with ProPress fittings. Copper repipes typically cost more and take a bit longer than PEX.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Montpelier, VT

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly durable piping material that's gaining popularity for repiping projects. CKV Plumbing specializes in PEX pipe replacement in Montpelier, VT, offering a system that resists freezing, corrosion, and scale buildup. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points. PEX also noticeably dampens water hammer noise, providing a quieter plumbing system for your home.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Montpelier, VT

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average home often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper typically costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 including access and restoration.     Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Montpelier, VT

    Many older homes in Montpelier, VT still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks. Our team specializes in the complete removal and replacement of these outdated systems with modern PEX or copper piping. This upgrade not only resolves existing issues but also greatly improves water quality and flow, boosting your home's value and plumbing reliability for decades to come.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Montpelier

    PEX-A tubing is flexible, resists scale buildup and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open up the full pipe diameter. Long continuous runs mean fewer joints in walls and attics. Most homes are repiped with PEX in two to four days.

    Repiping for Hard Water Areas in Montpelier, VT

    Town water systems in Montpelier and other communities are generally reliable, but older service lines can add rust and discolored water. Our repipe specialists consider local water conditions, recommending materials like PEX or properly installed copper that are more resistant to mineral accumulation, supporting a longer-lasting plumbing system.
