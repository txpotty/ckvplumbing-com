    Locating and Repairing Slab Leaks in Montpelier, VT

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Montpelier, VT

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is finished level with the floor.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Montpelier

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. A reroute bypasses it. The bad run stays in the ground unused while a new line goes overhead or in the walls, where it can be reached later. Montpelier homes with original plumbing see this often.

    Spot Repair Through the Slab in Montpelier, VT

    Spot repairs fit leaks in otherwise good pipe. One cut, one fix. The floor gets opened, the pipe replaced and tested, and the trench compacted and patched, ready for your flooring contractor.

    Slab Leak Reroute in Montpelier

    Older copper under a slab tends to leak again. A reroute stops the cycle. New PEX runs through the attic and down the walls while the old pipe stays buried, with openings placed behind vanities or in closets.

    Sewer Line Slab Leak Repair in Montpelier, VT

    Drain lines under the slab leak too. Those leaks smell. We run a sewer camera through the line to find the cracked fitting, belly or root intrusion, then repair it by tunneling, cutting the slab or lining the pipe, depending on where the damage is and how long it runs. Health codes in Montpelier, VT cover this work.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Montpelier, VT

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. That is when a repipe pays off. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Montpelier homes.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Montpelier, VT

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can adjust the pump to cut wear.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Montpelier

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Montpelier home.
