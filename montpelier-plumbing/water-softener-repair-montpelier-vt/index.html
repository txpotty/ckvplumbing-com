    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Montpelier, VT

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Montpelier

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. After a capacity test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced, and a good tank and valve with new resin performs like new. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Water Softener Leak Repair in Montpelier, VT

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Montpelier

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Water Softener Repair vs. Replacement in Montpelier, VT

    Age and parts availability decide whether a softener is worth repairing. CKV Plumbing gives honest advice on both counts. A sound tank with a rebuildable valve is worth saving, but an obsolete head without parts usually is not. We lay out both options.
