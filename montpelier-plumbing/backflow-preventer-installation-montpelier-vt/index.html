    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Montpelier

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In Montpelier, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers carefully install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Montpelier, VT

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Montpelier, VT, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a popular and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Montpelier

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules permit. We size them to avoid excessive pressure loss on your system.

    Backflow Testing and Certification in Montpelier

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it right away with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.
