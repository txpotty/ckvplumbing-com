    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Brattleboro

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one while the old pipe is fractured outward. This creates a new, seamless and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Brattleboro that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Brattleboro

    Some older homes still have Orangeburg fiber pipe or vitrified clay sewer laterals. Orangeburg flattens and blisters over time, while clay cracks and separates at joints.
