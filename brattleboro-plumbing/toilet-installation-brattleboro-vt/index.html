    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Brattleboro, VT

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Brattleboro

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. We keep common two-piece toilets on the truck.     Smart Toilet Installation in Brattleboro, VT

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most require a GFCI outlet within reach of the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Brattleboro

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Brattleboro, VT

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Brattleboro, VT

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Brattleboro

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. We check static pressure at a hose bib before recommending one, and we warn customers about the noise, since the flush is loud enough to hear across the house. They rarely clog.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Installation in Brattleboro, VT

    Wall-hung toilets need a wall at least 2 by 6 deep, or a furred-out chase built in front of an existing 2 by 4 wall. Floors stay clear underneath. That makes mopping easier and lets you set the bowl height anywhere within the carrier's range, which is handy for tall households or anyone who wants a comfort-height seat without a tall bowl. Service happens through the flush plate.
