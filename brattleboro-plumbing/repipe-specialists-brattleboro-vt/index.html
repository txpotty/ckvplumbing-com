    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Brattleboro

    Slab leaks are a serious and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations. Most Vermont homes have full basements, many with fieldstone or older foundations that can let in groundwater during wet seasons. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use modern detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.
