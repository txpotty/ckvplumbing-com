    How We Install Showers in Brattleboro, VT

    New Construction Shower Installation in Brattleboro, VT

    New homes get their shower plumbing during framing. We rough in valves, supply lines and drains once the walls are framed, set each valve to the depth of the planned finish and locate the drain for the pan you picked. Inspection comes next. Trim goes on once tile is done.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Brattleboro, VT

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. We pull the tub, move the drain from the end of the tub to where the shower pan needs it and raise the valve to shower height, usually around 48 inches off the floor. Slab homes need concrete work. The new shower is safer to step into.

    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Brattleboro

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support. Acrylic and steel tubs get mortar or foam underneath, while cast iron rests on a level ledger, which keeps the caulk line intact. Then the valve. We set the spout and head heights to your layout and test the overflow gasket with water.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Brattleboro

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. We pressure-test the valve and flood the pan for a day before tile.

    Shower Stall Installation in Brattleboro

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base. We set fiberglass and acrylic bases in a bed of mortar or setting compound so the floor of the unit does not flex underfoot, since flex is what cracks a base within a couple of years. The drain connects from below. Flow and temperature get tested after trim.

    Accessible and ADA Shower Installation in Brattleboro, VT

    Accessible showers start with blocking in the wall. Blocking goes in for grab bars, the valve goes where a seated person can reach it and a handheld goes on a slide bar. No high curbs. We follow ADA guidelines when you ask.

    Shower Valve and Trim Installation in Brattleboro, VT

    The valve sits behind the tile for decades. Each valve is set to the depth its trim requires, and we include integral stops whenever the valve supports them. Trim goes on after tile. That timing keeps the trim from getting scratched or caked with thinset while the tile is set.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Brattleboro

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. The generator goes within the maker's distance limit, and we plumb its supply, drain and steam line to the head. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.
