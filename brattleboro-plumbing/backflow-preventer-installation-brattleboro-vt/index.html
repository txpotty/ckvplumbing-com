    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Brattleboro

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Brattleboro. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. CKV Plumbing focuses on precise RPZ installation, with proper sizing, placement and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.
