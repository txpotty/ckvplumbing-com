    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. The vent is plain PVC.     Commercial Tankless Replacement in Brattleboro

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Brattleboro, VT

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Tankless-to-Tankless Replacement in Brattleboro

    Replacing an existing tankless with a new one is usually the simpler job, since the gas line, vent path and water connections are already there. We confirm they are sized right for the new unit. Corroded service valves get swapped while we are there.
