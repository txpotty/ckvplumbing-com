    Household sewers clog slowly. Grease, hair and wipes build up over months. Debris catches at a rough joint or a sag, and every load of laundry adds to it until the line closes, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Root Removal From Sewer Lines in Brattleboro

    Trees send roots toward any moisture they can find. Leaky joints feed them all year. Our root saw cuts the mass loose, and heavy growth gets a jetter pass to clean out the fine hairs, and a camera shows where roots are entering so you can choose between lining and repair.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Brattleboro, VT

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. Guessing costs money. The camera feed shows roots, grease, breaks and bellies in real time, and the locator marks the spot on the ground above.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Brattleboro, VT

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Brattleboro
