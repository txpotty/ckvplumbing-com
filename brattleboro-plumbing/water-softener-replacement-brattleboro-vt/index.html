    Softener Systems We Replace in Brattleboro, VT

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Brattleboro

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. A sound tank can keep its resin and get a new valve. Sometimes the whole unit goes.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Brattleboro

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Brattleboro

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. They cost more.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Brattleboro

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break them up and clean out the mush at the bottom. Cracked tanks and stuck safety floats get replaced. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Brattleboro

    Salt-based softeners pull calcium and magnesium out of the water by trading them for sodium on a bed of resin beads, which is the only method that actually makes water soft. Old clock-timer units come out and metered valves go in. You will buy less salt. The brine setting gets matched to your hardness so the unit is not wasting salt with every cycle.

    Commercial Softener Replacement in Brattleboro

    Espresso machines and commercial dishwashers scale up fast on hard water, and the service bills for descaling them are steep. We size commercial softeners to flow rate and daily gallons. Sometimes a carbon filter goes ahead of it.

    Softener Loop Installation in Brattleboro, VT

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Replacing Fleck Water Softeners in Brattleboro, VT

    Fleck control valves, the 5600SXT and 9100SXT in particular, are on a large share of the softeners we see in Vermont homes. If the valve is old enough that parts are hard to find, a new valve on a new tank is the smarter buy. Newer Fleck heads drop right in.
