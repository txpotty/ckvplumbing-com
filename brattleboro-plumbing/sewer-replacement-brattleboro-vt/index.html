    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Brattleboro

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. Over decades it scales up inside and the bottom wears through. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Brattleboro

    A sewer camera on a push cable goes in through the cleanout. The footage shows each joint, crack and root intrusion. A small transmitter called a sonde sits behind the camera head, so we can find its exact spot and depth from the yard above, which keeps the digging small. Bring questions.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Brattleboro

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all failed for different reasons. Modern PVC does not rust, flatten or let roots in at glued joints. Schedule 40 or SDR 35 PVC is what most codes call for on a lateral, choosing the wall thickness based on how deep it sits and what drives over it. Glued joints stay sealed.

    Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Brattleboro, VT

    Clay pipe came in short sections with a joint every two or three feet. That is a lot of joints for roots to find. Orangeburg is worse, since the tar-paper wall flattens slowly under the dirt above it, and both are good candidates for bursting because they break apart easily. Fast pulls.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Brattleboro

    Digging is still the right method for a lot of lines. A collapsed pipe cannot guide a bursting head. Sharp bends, a belly that needs regrading, or a route that should change all point toward an open trench, and we restore the sod or gravel afterward. Grass grows back.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Brattleboro, VT

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. We dig a launch pit near the house and a receiving pit near the main. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, which puts new pipe in the same spot without digging the yard. Driveways stay put.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Brattleboro, VT

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. CIPP lining coats the inside of the existing pipe instead. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, because it leaves you with brand new pipe rather than a liner inside an old one, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.
