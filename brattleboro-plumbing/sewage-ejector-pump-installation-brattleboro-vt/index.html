    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are different. CKV Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in Brattleboro. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly misinstallations.
