    Burst Copper Pipe Repair in Brattleboro, VT

    Copper in an exterior wall or attic can split when the water inside freezes. Keeping heat on, insulating vulnerable lines and letting faucets drip during deep cold are standard habits for homeowners here. Close the main valve. The split often does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many bursts are found hours after the cold. We cut out the split and solder in new pipe. Then we add insulation.

    Solder Joint Leak Repair in Brattleboro, VT

    Older solder joints can fail from poor original work, thermal expansion or vibration. Look for white or green crust. A leaking joint usually weeps for a while before it drips, and we clean, reflux and resolder the joint or replace the fitting with new pipe on each side when the old copper is too thin. Then we pressure test it.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Brattleboro, VT

    Copper gives warning signs before it leaks. Most people miss them. Green crust at fittings, a stain on a basement ceiling and high static pressure all point to trouble ahead, so we look over the pipe you can see and test the pressure. A regulator often helps.

    Under Slab Copper Leak Repair in Brattleboro, VT

    Copper lines under a slab can wear through where they rub concrete or gravel. Warm floors are a clue. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment, so you can pick between a targeted floor repair and a reroute overhead. We quote each way.

    Commercial Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Brattleboro

    Commercial buildings often run larger diameter copper for domestic water. Shutdowns cost money. We repair copper supply lines in offices, restaurants and apartment buildings, using press tools on large lines to shorten the shutdown and skip the torch. Early or late work is available.

    Copper Fitting and Valve Repair in Brattleboro

    Angle stops, shutoff valves and copper fittings under sinks and behind toilets can weep or seize. Minerals jam them. Hard water buildup inside a valve can keep it from closing when you need it most, and quarter-turn valves go in to replace the old multi-turn stops. We test each one.

    Underground Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Brattleboro

    Buried copper service lines can leak for months before anyone notices. Bills tell the story. We locate the leak with acoustic gear and tracer gas, then repair it through a small pit or replace the line by pulling new PEX or Type K copper through the old path. Every repair gets a pressure test.
