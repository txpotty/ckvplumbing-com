    Well Pump Problems We Fix in Brattleboro, VT

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Brattleboro, VT

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can quickly swell rivers in Vermont, and many basements take on water during the wet season. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. An arrestor costs little.

    Above-Ground Jet Pump Repair in Brattleboro

    Lost prime is the number one jet pump complaint. These pumps can also leak at the shaft seal or wear out an impeller. We reprime the pump, replace the seal and check the foot valve and suction line for leaks, since one tiny air leak will undo the whole repair. Everything is above ground.

    Low Water Pressure Repair in Brattleboro

    Weak pressure on a well is not always the pump. A clogged sediment filter, a pressure switch set too low or a leak in the yard line can all cause it. Sometimes the pump is fine and the well is simply producing less water than the house is asking for during peak use. We test each one.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Brattleboro

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. CKV Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Brattleboro, VT well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Pressure Tank Repair in Brattleboro, VT

    A waterlogged pressure tank makes the pump short cycle, turning on and off every few seconds. That rapid cycling burns out pumps early. We check the air charge, inspect the bladder and recharge or replace the tank, then confirm the pump runs at least a minute per cycle. Steadier pressure follows.

    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Brattleboro

    Submersible pumps hang inside the well casing on drop pipe, often 100 feet or more below the wellhead. The usual signs are no water at all, weak pressure or a hum with no flow. We test the motor from the surface first. When a pull is needed, a hoist brings the pipe up safely so we can replace the motor, the impellers or the check valve.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Brattleboro, VT

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. Our technicians know them well, from the QD control boxes to the SubDrive constant pressure units. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. Replacing a failed motor usually costs less than a rewind once shipping is counted.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Brattleboro, VT

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. The control box capacitor and relay are next on the list. Our technicians test circuits with the power locked out. All wiring and splices meet Vermont electrical code before the pump restarts.
