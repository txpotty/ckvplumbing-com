    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Brattleboro

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Brattleboro

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Soft water reaches every fixture. We can skip the hose bibs.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Brattleboro

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one.     Water Softener Replacement in Brattleboro, VT

    Older softeners lose ground as resin wears out, valve seals leak or injectors clog. While the old system is out, we inspect the loop and the drain, then install a newer metered unit. Metered valves save salt. Performance comes back right away.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Brattleboro

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning.     Combination Softener and Carbon Filter Systems in Brattleboro, VT

    City water often carries chlorine or chloramine that affects taste and smell. A carbon filter placed ahead of the softener removes those chemicals and protects the resin, since chlorine slowly breaks resin beads down over the years. Taste improves fast. Setups can be two tanks or one dual-stage tank.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Brattleboro

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. A new metered unit often uses far less salt and water than an older model on a timer, which shows up on both bills. That pays back over time. We install several models.
