    Kitchen Drain Cleaning in Brattleboro

    Grease is the big one in kitchens. Hot grease cools in the line and hardens on the pipe wall. Food scraps and soap stick to that layer and the opening keeps shrinking until the sink holds water, and we clear it with a drum machine that is safe for PVC and old cast iron alike.

    Camera Inspection After Drain Cleaning in Brattleboro

    A camera shows what the cable felt. Self-leveling heads keep the picture upright. In cast iron we often find scale, channeling along the bottom of the pipe and cracked hubs, while clay lines show offset joints and root entry, and a locator marks the depth on the surface so a repair can be dug in the right spot.

    Scheduled Drain Maintenance in Brattleboro, VT

    Roots grow back. Cutting them buys about a year. For homes with mature trees over the sewer line, a yearly cleaning keeps the roots small and the line open, which is cheaper than an emergency call and lets you plan for a repair on your own timeline instead of the pipe's.

    Main Sewer Drain Cleaning in Brattleboro

    Tub filling when the toilet flushes is the classic main line sign. Find the cleanout, and the job gets easier. Ours is a sectional cable with a cutting head, fed through the 3 or 4 inch line until it breaks through, then recommend a camera pass to look for a break, and if there is no cleanout at all we can add one.

    Outdoor & Storm Drain Cleaning in Brattleboro

    Yard drains and downspout lines fill with leaves, grit and roots. Ice dams and frozen ground in late winter can direct meltwater toward foundations, making a working sump pump important. Standing water near the foundation is the warning sign. We jet or cable the line to the outlet and pull the debris out of the catch basin, so the next storm runs off instead of soaking into the soil next to the house.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Brattleboro, VT

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A sectional cable is built to chop and pull a clog out. A jetter scrubs the pipe wall clean and flushes the debris downstream to the city main or septic tank, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Restaurant and Shop Drains in Brattleboro, VT

    Commercial kitchens fight fats, oils and grease every day. Even with an interceptor, grease builds up downstream. A regular jetting schedule, set by how much frying your kitchen does, keeps the line open, and we leave a service report you can show the health inspector or the landlord.

Our tech cables the standpipe and its branch, and checks the trap and vent while there, and garage floor drains full of grit and gravel get cleared and flushed so they can take a full washer discharge without backing up.
