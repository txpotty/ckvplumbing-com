    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in Middlebury, VT

    Check Valve and Discharge Line Service in Middlebury, VT

    The check valve stops pumped water from falling back into the pit when the pump shuts off. A failed valve makes the pump run twice as often and wears out the motor. We test and replace check valves and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose joints. We also make sure water exits away from the house so it does not soak into the soil near the slab or foundation.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Middlebury, VT

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to support reliable operation for your home in Middlebury, VT.

    Wayne Sump Pump Maintenance in Middlebury, VT

    For Wayne sump pump owners in Middlebury, VT, CKV Plumbing offers expert maintenance.     Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Middlebury, VT

    The float switch is the most frequent reason a sump pump fails to start. Tethered floats can tangle on the pump body, and vertical floats can stick from mineral crust or debris. We clean or replace the switch and set the on and off levels so the pump does not short cycle. Electronic sensor switches are an option for tight pits where floats get hung up.

    Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in Middlebury

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements. We test duplex systems, alternating controls and high-water alarms on a scheduled basis. Maintenance logs help property managers stay on top of inspections.

    Many Middlebury, VT homeowners rely on battery backup sump pumps for protection during power outages. CKV Plumbing provides essential maintenance for these critical systems, including testing the battery's charge and health, inspecting the backup pump's operation and checking the charging system.
