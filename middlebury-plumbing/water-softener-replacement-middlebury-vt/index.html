    Traditional Salt-Based Water Softener Replacement in Middlebury

    Salt-based water softeners are a common and effective solution for hard water in Middlebury. These systems use ion exchange to remove calcium and magnesium minerals, which are notorious for causing scale buildup. We replace old, inefficient salt-based units with modern, high-efficiency models that use less salt and water. Our plumbers ensure proper brine tank installation and regeneration settings for strong performance.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Middlebury

    Cracked brine tanks, salt bridges and failing float assemblies cause softeners to stop regenerating properly. We replace brine tanks and safety floats to restore consistent brine draw. We also clean out mushy salt buildup that clogs the tank. A new brine tank is often part of a full system replacement. We recommend the best salt type for your system.

    Whole-Home Water Softener System Upgrades in Middlebury

    Upgrading your whole-home water softener system in Middlebury is a significant investment that pays off in the long run. Older systems may struggle with the mineral content in your water or simply be undersized for your household's needs.
