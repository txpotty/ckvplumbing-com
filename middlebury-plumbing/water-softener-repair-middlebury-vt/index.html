    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Middlebury

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Control Valve Repair in Middlebury

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. CKV Plumbing technicians rebuild these valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model. Timing problems get fixed, whether mechanical or electronic. Common parts are on the truck, so most valve repairs are finished the same day without a second trip to the supply house.

    Softener Not Regenerating in Middlebury

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. We test each part. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Middlebury, VT

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Older brine tanks can crack, and we repair or replace those too. Fill the tank halfway.

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads so they cannot grab hardness. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. Fresh resin brings capacity back.
