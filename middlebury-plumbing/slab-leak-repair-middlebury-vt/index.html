    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Middlebury, VT

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most practical repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section. Our team makes sure the repair is fully compliant with Vermont plumbing codes and the slab is correctly restored, preventing future issues. We take real care to contain dust and debris during this process in your Middlebury home.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Middlebury

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This removes future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Middlebury with aging copper or galvanized pipes.
