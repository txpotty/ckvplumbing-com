    Expansion Tank Installation in Middlebury, VT

    Many homes have a pressure reducing valve or check valve that creates a closed system. When water heats and expands, pressure can rise and stress pipes and relief valves. An expansion tank absorbs that extra pressure. We install and pre-charge the tank to match your water pressure. This protects your new water heater and pipes.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Middlebury

    Many plumbing codes require the installation of an expansion tank with a new water heater, especially in closed-loop systems. Plumbing in Vermont follows state plumbing rules based on a national model code with state amendments, and the state licenses plumbers. An expansion tank protects your water heater and plumbing system from excessive pressure buildup, which can be caused by the heating process. Our installation service includes correctly sizing and installing the expansion tank to support proper system operation and prevent premature wear on your new water heater and other plumbing fixtures in Middlebury.
