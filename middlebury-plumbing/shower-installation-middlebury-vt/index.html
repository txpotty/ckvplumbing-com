    Custom Shower System Installation in Middlebury, VT

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. One head works fine off a standard valve, while multiple outlets need a thermostatic valve, volume controls and larger 3/4-inch supply lines. We plan the layout first. Then we check whether the water heater can keep up.

    Shower Stall Installation in Middlebury

    A one-piece stall needs a level, fully supported base.
