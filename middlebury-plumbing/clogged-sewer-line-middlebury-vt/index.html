    Hydro-Jetting Services in Middlebury, VT

    Hydro-jetting is a powerful method for clearing severe sewer line clogs, especially those caused by grease, scale, or heavy sludge buildup. This process uses highly pressurized water jets to scour the inside of your pipes, not only clearing the blockage but also cleaning the pipe walls. It is an excellent preventative maintenance tool and is especially effective for older pipes in Middlebury that may have collected years of debris. This technique is safe for most pipe materials.

    Cleaning vs. Repairing a Sewer Line in Middlebury, VT

    If the camera shows a sound pipe with a grease or root clog, cleaning is usually all you need. Cracked, offset or collapsed pipe will keep clogging no matter how often it is cleared.
