    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Middlebury, VT

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit.     Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Middlebury

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. For a silent unit we test the breaker, reset and switch, and for a humming one we check the flywheel and motor before deciding if it is worth repairing.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Middlebury

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different. That whine often comes from worn motor bearings, and once bearings start to fail the noise only gets worse until the motor seizes. Most smells start at the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Middlebury

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most electrical faults are easy once found.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Middlebury

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. The red reset button on the bottom is the first stop, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. In that damp cabinet, wire nuts corrode and cord connections loosen, so we replace them instead of retightening.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Middlebury, VT

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed.
