    Water Line Replacement vs. Repair in Middlebury, VT

    Deciding between pipe repair and full replacement depends on several factors, including the age of your pipes, the extent of the damage, and the frequency of past issues. A small, isolated leak might be repaired, but widespread corrosion or multiple leaks usually warrant replacement. CKV Plumbing provides straightforward assessments in Middlebury, VT, explaining the pros and cons of each option.     Copper Pinhole Leak Replacement in Middlebury, VT

    Pinhole leaks in copper usually start as a small green stain or a damp spot on drywall. They are caused by a mix of water chemistry, high velocity and thin Type M pipe, and once they begin they tend to keep coming back. Patching each leak costs $300 to $800 per visit plus drywall repair. We can replace the affected branch or repipe the home with PEX, which does not develop pinholes. We help you compare the cost of both paths.

    Burst Pipe Repair & Replacement in Middlebury, VT

    Frozen pipes are a common winter problem in Vermont, especially in older homes with plumbing in exterior walls or unheated spaces. Cold snaps can lead to burst pipes, especially in exposed areas or unheated spaces. When a pipe bursts in your Middlebury, VT home, it causes immediate water damage and requires urgent attention. Our team provides emergency service to shut off water, remove damaged sections, and replace them with new, more freeze-resistant piping, often PEX, to prevent future occurrences. We work fast to minimize disruption and water loss.

    PEX Repiping in Middlebury

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale buildup and expands slightly if water freezes, which helps in colder homes. We use expansion fittings that keep the full inside diameter of the pipe for better flow. Systems can be run as a home-run manifold with a shutoff for each fixture or as a trunk and branch layout. PEX installs faster than copper with many fewer fittings in the walls. It is our most requested repipe material.
