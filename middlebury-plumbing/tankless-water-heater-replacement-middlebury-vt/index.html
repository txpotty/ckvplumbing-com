    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Middlebury, VT

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Middlebury

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. We replace Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart units. Sometimes a heat pump tank serves the house better, and we will say so.

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Middlebury, VT

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. Seasonal camps and vacation homes are usually drained and winterized each fall to keep pipes from bursting while the place sits empty. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. Often the simplest job we do.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Middlebury

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. Most need at least a 3/4-inch line. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Middlebury, VT

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Built-in pumps on Navien A and some Rinnai models push water through a crossover valve or a dedicated return. The pump runs on a timer. Mornings and evenings, usually.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Middlebury, VT

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. A line that holds pressure at rest can still sag under full load. Both readings get taken.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Middlebury
