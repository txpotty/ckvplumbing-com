    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Middlebury, VT

    Pool auto-fill lines and hose connections give chlorinated water a straight path back to the supply.     Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Middlebury

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Middlebury. We install Watts and Febco units.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Middlebury, VT

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Middlebury, VT. A spring-loaded air inlet opens when the pressure falls, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible. Clearance and drainage get checked as well.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in Middlebury

    Double check assemblies work for low-hazard connections such as many fire lines and some irrigation. They have two spring-loaded checks and test cocks for annual certification, and since they do not discharge water, they can go in a vault or below grade where local rules allow, sized so the pressure loss stays small. Flow drives the size.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in Middlebury, VT

    Above-ground assemblies bake in summer sun and can freeze on a cold night. Keeping heat on, insulating vulnerable lines and letting faucets drip during deep cold are standard habits for homeowners here. Depending on exposure, we add an insulated enclosure or a heated cabinet, and a locking enclosure also stops scrap thieves, who target brass assemblies because they are easy to unbolt and sell by weight in Middlebury, VT. There is space inside for the tester.
