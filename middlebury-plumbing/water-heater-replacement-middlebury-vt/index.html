    What Goes Into a Water Heater Swap in Middlebury, VT

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Middlebury, VT

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need space and airflow, often a room of about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they work best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Conventional Tank Water Heater Replacement in Middlebury

    Standard tank heaters remain the most common type, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment. A leak at the base, loud popping or rusty hot water means it is time, and a like-for-like or resized swap usually takes two to four hours. You shower tonight.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Middlebury

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is common, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. A whole-home tankless unit can draw four times the gas of a 40-gallon tank, so the gas line from the meter usually has to be upsized, and the old metal vent gets replaced with stainless or PVC run to the maker's specs. You see both numbers.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Middlebury

    Older homes often still have galvanized supply lines, cast iron drains or even lead service lines that deserve inspection and replacement. Attic water heaters take more work to replace than ones in a garage or closet. Moving a 50-gallon tank through a pull-down ladder or small opening takes two people, and code wants a pan with a drain line, a platform and a light. Two-person crew, always.

    Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Middlebury, VT

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on the majority of our swaps. Bradford White is sold only through plumbers and known for durability, Rheem has a wide lineup and good parts support, and AO Smith offers many sizes and efficiency options, so the right pick usually depends on fit, warranty and stock. Any of the three works.

    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Middlebury

    Bradford White sells through professional plumbers only, so you will not find one at a big-box store. They hold up well, and warranty support runs through the installing plumber, which makes a future claim easy for us to handle. Parts are easy to get.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Middlebury

    Businesses in Middlebury put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. Downtime costs money.

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Middlebury

    Electric water heaters in Middlebury usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. Elements are cheap to swap. Tank leaks are the end, so we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to your existing circuit. Hot water is back the same day.
