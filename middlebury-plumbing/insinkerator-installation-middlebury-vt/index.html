    Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Middlebury

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. Each type gets wired its own way.

    First-Time Disposal Installation in Middlebury

    Kitchens that never had a disposal need some rework. The sink needs a disposal flange. Then the drain gets reworked, and a switched outlet goes in, with an electrician if there is no power in the cabinet yet. We also check the drain slope to the stack.

    Evolution Series Installation in Middlebury

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. The Evolution Septic Assist injects an enzyme solution into the grind chamber each time it runs, which helps break down food waste before it reaches the tank. The enzyme cartridge gets mounted, and we show you the refill. Scraping plates into the trash still helps.
