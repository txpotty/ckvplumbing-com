    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Middlebury, VT

    Many older homes in Middlebury, VT have copper water lines beneath their foundations, which can corrode over time due to soil conditions or water chemistry. Our technicians fix or replace sections of damaged copper piping, ensuring a durable seal that holds up to pressure and ground movement and helps prevent future leaks.

    Foundation Leak Detection in Middlebury

    Accurate detection is the most important step in foundation leak repair. We isolate hot and cold lines and pressure test them to verify a leak exists. Then we use acoustic listening equipment and electronic line tracing to find the exact spot beneath the foundation. Thermal imaging helps confirm hot water leaks by showing warm spots on the floor.

    Water Line Reroute in Middlebury

    Rerouting abandons the leaking line under the foundation and runs a new line through the attic or walls. This avoids breaking concrete and removes the risk of future leaks under the slab. We usually use PEX for reroutes because it is flexible and resists corrosion. Drywall openings are kept small and neat for simple patching.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Middlebury, VT

    Crawl space foundations can suffer leaks from plumbing, groundwater, or HVAC condensation. These leaks can lead to wood rot, mold, and structural damage if not addressed quickly. Our team carefully inspects the crawl space and identifies the source of water intrusion. We then put in place solutions such as pipe repair, sump pump installation, or vapor barrier replacement to dry out and protect your crawl space in Middlebury, VT from future moisture issues.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Middlebury, VT

    For homes with basements in Middlebury, VT, wall leaks can be a real concern. We offer complete basement waterproofing services, including interior and exterior sealing. This may involve applying waterproof coatings, injecting cracks with polyurethane or epoxy, or setting up interior drainage systems. These measures prevent water from penetrating porous concrete and masonry, keeping your basement dry and healthy.

    Post-Tension Slab Leak Repair in Middlebury

    Many newer homes are built on post-tension slabs with steel cables under high tension. Cutting into these slabs requires care and scanning to avoid hitting a cable. We use ground-penetrating radar or other scanning methods before opening the floor. When the risk is too great, rerouting the plumbing is usually the safer option.
