    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Middlebury

    Businesses in Middlebury use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns. Backflow devices, heaters, flush valves, floor drains, trap primers and the sewer all get checked. Downtime costs money. With a ranked report, repairs get scheduled after close instead of forcing a shutdown.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Middlebury, VT

    A long, cold heating season is the defining stress on Vermont plumbing, with months of freezing nights and repeated freeze and thaw cycles. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. Insulation gets checked too. Fixing a cracked hose bibb or adding insulation is a small job compared to a burst pipe in the wall behind it.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Middlebury

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. We document each unit. We note heaters, fixtures and sewers by unit, and commercial jobs can include backflow and grease lines, giving owners a budget for repairs.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Middlebury, VT

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. We flush the water heater, exercise the main shutoff and angle stops, check pressure and look for slow drains and weeping fittings. It takes about an hour. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Middlebury, VT

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. We identify copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, polybutylene and Kitec, and we note the known failure patterns for each one. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Middlebury, VT

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Stops and the main valve tend to seize with age, and gurgling drains can mean a vent problem, both of which are simple fixes once found.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Middlebury

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. Meter tests, pressure loss checks and a look for damp baseboards come first, and slab homes get acoustic and thermal follow-up. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Water Heater Inspections in Middlebury

    Water heaters give clues long before they fail. We read the serial number for age, look for rust at the fittings and the base, check the venting and test the T and P relief valve. Tankless units get checked for scale. On gas units, we look at the burner and flame, check for a sediment trap and confirm the vent is drafting so combustion gases go outside.
