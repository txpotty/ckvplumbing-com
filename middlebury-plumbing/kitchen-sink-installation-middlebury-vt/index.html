    How We Install Kitchen Sinks in Middlebury, VT: Style by Style

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Middlebury

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and a lower gauge means thicker steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Mounting style changes the plumbing. We connect the strainers, faucet and disposal either way.

    Farmhouse Sink Plumbing in Middlebury, VT

    Fireclay and cast iron farmhouse sinks can weigh well over 100 pounds empty, before a single drop of water goes in. We build a support frame inside the cabinet and set the sink level before the counter is installed. A deep bowl means a lower trap. The drain gets planned for that depth from day one.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Middlebury, VT

    Every fabricator handles undermount clips a bit differently. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. The bowl is braced regardless. The trap does not get connected until the adhesive cures.

    Double Bowl Sink and Continuous Waste in Middlebury

    A double bowl drains both sides through one trap, using either a center outlet continuous waste or an end outlet tee. We pick the layout around your disposal and dishwasher, since a disposal on one side changes where the tee can go and how much fall each side gets. Heavy-wall fittings resist leaks.

    Disposal and Dishwasher Hookup with Sink Installation in Middlebury, VT

    Most new kitchen sinks get paired with a disposal and a dishwasher. The disposal gets mounted, the dishwasher drain goes through an air gap or high loop, and the dishwasher gets its own hot line and shutoff. Doing it all at once saves money. We test it all the way you will use it.

    Kitchen Sink Installation Cost Factors in Middlebury, VT

    A drop-in swap costs less than an undermount on stone. Each extra, like a new disposal, a moved drain, a lowered wall stub or replacing galvanized stubs, adds labor and shows up as its own line on the quote. Plumbing is priced apart from the counter.

    Stainless Sinks With Sound Pads in Middlebury

    Budget stainless sinks ring like a drum when dishes hit them or the disposal runs.     Undermount Sink Installation in Middlebury, VT

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.
