    Drains We Replace in Middlebury, VT

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Middlebury, VT

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Middlebury

    Traps leak at the joints first. The beveled washers inside the slip nuts flatten and harden over the years, and a trap that has been taken apart several times to clear clogs often has stripped plastic threads or a cracked nut. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.

    Emergency Sink Drain Service in Middlebury

    A trap that falls apart sends a sink of water into the cabinet. Turn off the faucet, set a bucket under it, and stop using that sink, and if the drain is backed up into a second fixture, stop using water elsewhere in the house too. A plumber comes out with trap kits. Most get fixed for good on the first visit.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Middlebury, VT

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Chemicals left sitting in a metal trap eat through it, so many old traps we pull have pinholes at the bottom. Plastic traps resist that damage. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Kitchen Drain and Disposal Tie-In in Middlebury

    Disposals change the kitchen drain. The outlet on the disposal has to sit above the trap inlet, the dishwasher hose needs a high loop or an air gap, and the trap has to line up without the slip joints pulling sideways. We set them all at once. Then the disposal runs with water while we watch.

    Trap Arm and Wall Drain Repair in Middlebury, VT

    Leaks right at the wall point to the trap arm or stub. A cracked trap arm or a stub rusted through behind the escutcheon will drip down the inside of the cabinet back panel, where you only notice it when the wood swells or the cabinet smells musty. The bad section gets cut back. Then new PVC gets tied in.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Middlebury

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. A 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout plug goes in. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.
