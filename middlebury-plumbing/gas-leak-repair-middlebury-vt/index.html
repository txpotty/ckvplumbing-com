    Gas Leak Detection in Middlebury

    Some gas leaks are obvious from the rotten egg smell, while others are slow and hard to find. We pressure test the system with a gauge to confirm a leak, then isolate sections to narrow it down.     Appliance Connector Replacement in Middlebury

    Flexible connectors on ranges, dryers and water heaters can crack, kink or loosen. Older uncoated brass connectors are a known hazard and should be swapped out.
