    Noritz Descaling in Middlebury, VT

    Noritz exchangers scale up fast on hard water. The descaler is one Noritz approves, and the cycle stays within the manual's time limit so the metal does not etch. The fan motor and exhaust path get checked while the pump runs.

    Commercial Tankless Maintenance in Middlebury, VT

    Commercial kitchens and salons burn through hot water all day, so their tankless units scale up much faster than a house unit. We test gas pressure under full fire, not at idle. We pull and clean the inlet screens and air filters. Downtime is the real cost. So we schedule the work before opening or after close.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Middlebury

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. On a Rinnai visit we clean the air intake filter and check the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Middlebury

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. The condensate trap gets cleared and the neutralizer media checked. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Multi-Unit Tankless Checks in Middlebury, VT

    Restaurants and salons often run two or more tankless heaters on a shared manifold, and one weak unit can drag the whole bank down. Gas pressure gets read with every unit firing together. Each heater gets its own flow and temperature reading. Then we compare them.

    Recirculation and Combi Unit Service in Middlebury, VT

    Built-in recirculation pumps and buffer tanks get hot water to the tap faster, but they also push more water through the exchanger every day, so those units scale sooner. The recirc loop gets flushed with the exchanger. Then we check whether the system uses a crossover valve or a dedicated return line and set the schedule to match.

    Home Tankless Flushing in Middlebury

    Some Vermont wells carry iron, manganese or other naturally occurring minerals, so a water test is the first step before choosing treatment. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. We hook a pump to the service valves and circulate descaling solution for about an hour. The flow returns. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.

    Rinnai Air Filter and Flame Rod Care in Middlebury

    Rinnai cabinets are tight. On these units we pull the air intake filter, wash it and check the flame rod for carbon buildup. On recirculating models we also check the pump schedule, because a wrong setting burns gas for nothing.
