    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. Handheld hoses and slide bars can go in during the same visit.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Middlebury, VT

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open.     Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Middlebury, VT

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. Each part gets tested. We also check the overflow gasket, a frequent hidden leak.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Middlebury

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. We replace the cartridge. Valves with no parts left get a replacement plan and an honest look at the tile.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Repair in Middlebury

    Most shower repairs, like a cartridge or diverter, cost $150 to $375. Valve replacements that require opening the wall usually run $450 to $1,200, depending on the valve and how much wall has to come open. Pan repairs cost more.

    Mineral scale in the head or the cartridge is the usual cause. A pressure-balance valve that sticks, a main valve left partly closed after other work or a problem at the water heater can also cut flow at the shower. We test each.

    Most cartridge and diverter repairs take one to two hours. Leak repairs that require opening a wall or rebuilding a pan take longer.

    Many do. Moen and Delta offer lifetime cartridge warranties to the original owner, and we can help you check whether yours is covered.

    Every major brand, plus a lot of older ones. Moen, Delta, Kohler, Pfister, American Standard and Grohe make up most of what we see, and we carry cartridges for the common models. Older brands may need a part ordered.

    Cartridge and showerhead swaps usually need no permit. Relocating supply lines or replacing long sections of pipe behind the tile can require one, depending on the rules where you live. We tell you before we start.

    Unscrew the head and soak it in vinegar first. Still weak? The cartridge or restrictor may need work.

    Watch for drips and temperature swings first. Low pressure, standing water at the drain, banging pipes when the handle closes and stains on the ceiling below all point to a problem worth a look. Catching it early saves money.
