    Recurring Drain Clogs and Camera Diagnosis in Middlebury, VT

    If the same drain clogs every few weeks, the problem is often a sagging pipe, a broken fitting or heavy scale. We run a small inspection camera through the line to see the inside of the pipe. You see the footage on screen with us. That lets you decide whether a deeper cleaning, a spot repair or a section replacement makes the most sense.

    Bathroom Sink and Vanity Drain Clogs in Middlebury, VT

    Bathroom sinks clog from hair, toothpaste and soap that bind together on the pop-up stopper and in the P-trap. Hard water can add a mineral crust that makes the pipe narrower over time. We pull and clean the stopper, clear the trap and run a small cable through the wall line. We can also replace worn pop-up assemblies and corroded tailpieces during the same visit.

    Commercial Drain Clog Clearing in Middlebury, VT

    Restaurants, salons and offices see heavy drain use and need fast, dependable service. We clear grease-heavy kitchen lines, hair-clogged salon sinks and mop sinks. Preventive jetting schedules are available to keep lines open between visits. We can work early or late to avoid interrupting your business.

    Kitchen Sink Drain Clogs in Middlebury

    Kitchen drains clog from cooking grease, coffee grounds and starchy food that stick to the pipe walls and harden. Garbage disposals can push food further down the line where it collects in horizontal runs. We remove the trap, run a cable through the trap arm and branch line and clear the buildup back to the main stack. For heavy grease, we may follow with a small jetter to wash the walls clear.

    Bathroom Sink & Shower Clog Repair in Middlebury, VT

    Hair, soap residue, and toothpaste are common culprits of bathroom drain clogs. CKV Plumbing provides thorough cleaning for these drains, using augers and sometimes chemical-free solutions to clear blockages.     Hydro-Jetting Services in Middlebury, VT

    For severe or recurring clogs, hydro-jetting is an excellent solution. This method uses high-pressure water streams to blast away buildup, grease, scale, and even tree roots from your pipes. It's a thorough cleaning process that leaves pipes scoured, reducing the likelihood of future blockages and extending pipe life, particularly in homes with hard water deposits.

    Outdoor Drain & Storm Drain Clearing in Middlebury, VT

    Outdoor drains, including those for patios, driveways, and storm drains, are crucial for preventing water damage. Ice dams and frozen ground in late winter can direct meltwater toward foundations, making a working sump pump important. These drains can become clogged with leaves, dirt, and debris. We provide complete cleaning and repair for outdoor drainage systems to ensure proper water runoff and protect your property's foundation in Middlebury.

    Main Sewer Line Clog Clearing in Middlebury, VT

    A clogged main sewer line can affect all drains in your home and is a serious issue. Causes often include tree root intrusion, pipe collapse, or significant grease buildup, common in older homes. We use advanced camera inspections to locate the blockage and hydro-jetting to powerfully clear it, restoring full drainage to your entire property.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Clogged Drain Repair in Middlebury

    The cost of clogged drain repair in Middlebury, VT can range from $150 to $500 for a standard clog, depending on severity and location. More complex issues like main line clogs requiring hydro-jetting or camera inspection might range from $400 to $1000 or more. We provide clear, upfront pricing after a thorough diagnosis.

    Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your pipes, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If multiple drains are affected, it often indicates a main line issue.

    We rely on professional equipment from trusted brands like Spartan Tool, RIDGID, and Gorlitz. These tools are known for their reliability and effectiveness in diagnosing and clearing various types of drain clogs, ensuring a high standard of service.

    Most single fixture clogs are cleared in 30 to 90 minutes. Lines with heavy buildup or limited access can take longer. We tell you if the job will run beyond the usual time.

    You should call a professional plumber for clogged drain repair if DIY methods like plungers are ineffective, if you experience multiple clogged drains, or if you suspect a main sewer line blockage. Recurring clogs or slow drains also indicate a need for professional diagnosis to prevent bigger issues.

    A plunger or a hand-held hair snake can handle many simple tub and sink clogs. If the clog returns, affects more than one fixture or does not respond to a plunger, call a plumber. Forcing a cable can crack old pipes or scratch finishes.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, CKV Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Middlebury. They often provide only temporary relief, can damage pipes over time, and pose environmental and health risks. Professional tools like augers or hydro-jetting are safer and more effective.

    For most standard clogged drain repairs within your home, permits are usually not required in Middlebury. However, if the repair involves excavation, replacing significant sections of the main sewer line, or altering the plumbing system's structure, a permit may be necessary.
