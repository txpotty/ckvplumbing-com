    Hydro Jetting for Main Sewer Lines in Middlebury

    Main sewer line clogs can be serious, leading to backups throughout your property. Our hydro jetting service targets these critical lines in Middlebury, blasting away stubborn tree roots, sludge, and other obstructions. Tree roots are a common problem, invading sewer lines as they seek moisture. We use root-cutting nozzles with our hydro jetters to break up roots and flush them out, restoring full capacity to your main sewer.

    Laundry Drain Jetting in Middlebury, VT

    Washing machines discharge lint, fabric softener residue and fine sand or grit. These build into a gray sludge that slows the standpipe and overflows during the spin cycle. Jetting clears the standpipe and branch line completely. We also check the trap, vent and drain size to make sure they can handle the washer flow. A lint filter on the discharge hose helps keep the line clean.

    Drain Jetting for Hard Water Scale in Middlebury

    Hard water leaves mineral deposits inside drain pipes, especially in older galvanized and cast iron. That rough scale traps hair, grease and soap, leading to frequent clogs. A jetting nozzle designed for scale can break up and wash away much of this buildup. We use the camera to judge how much wall remains before and after. Clean pipe drains better and clogs less often.

    Drain Jetting Cost Versus Snaking in Middlebury, VT

    Jetting a branch line costs more than a quick snaking visit. But if you are calling for the same clog several times a year, jetting typically saves money over time. It removes the cause instead of just opening a path. For a one-time hair clog, a cable may be all you need. We recommend the option that suits your situation and show you both prices.
