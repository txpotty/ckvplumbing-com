    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Middlebury

    A properly working temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank.     Anode Rod Inspection and Replacement in Middlebury, VT

    The anode rod, often called the sacrificial rod, is vital for preventing corrosion inside your hot water tank. Water chemistry varies from one neighborhood to the next, and it changes the rod's lifespan. Our technicians inspect the anode rod for wear and recommend replacement if it's significantly corroded, typically every 3 to 5 years. Replacing a worn anode rod is a cost-effective way to protect your tank from rust and leaks, sparing you a full tank replacement.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Middlebury

    A standard flush and inspection typically runs $125 to $250 depending on tank size and access. Anode replacement usually adds $75 to $200 in parts and labor, and powered anodes cost more.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend yearly hot water tank maintenance. This is especially true where water is hard, since minerals speed up sediment buildup and anode rod depletion.

    Routine hot water tank maintenance, such as flushing and inspections, usually does not require a permit in Middlebury. However, if major components are replaced or the entire unit is swapped out, a plumbing permit may be necessary. Our team handles all necessary permit applications.
