    Sump Pit Installation in Middlebury, VT

    Pit size controls how often the pump starts. Too small a pit means the pump short cycles in heavy flow, so we usually install an 18 by 22 inch or 18 by 30 inch basin with a solid bottom and perforated sides wrapped in filter fabric. Fewer starts mean a longer life.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Installation in Middlebury

    Rigid PVC carries the water from the pump out through the rim joist and at least 10 feet from the house. We add a spring check valve so the water in the pipe cannot fall back, which cuts motor run time and the loud thunk you hear after every cycle. Frozen pipes are a common winter problem in Vermont, especially in older homes with plumbing in exterior walls or unheated spaces. We fit a freeze-resistant outlet.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Middlebury

    Zoeller's M53 Mighty-Mate is the cast iron pump we install more than any other. It runs at 3/10 horsepower on a vertical float switch, and it moves about 2,580 gallons an hour at 5 feet of lift, enough for a lot of Middlebury basements. Parts are easy to get.

    Duplex systems with two pumps and an alternating panel are standard for parking garages and multi-unit buildings with below-grade levels in Middlebury. The panel swaps the lead pump every cycle, so wear stays even. Staff get a high water alarm. We coordinate the wiring with your electrician.
