    Standard tank heaters are still in most homes, and they usually fail from age, rust or sediment.     Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith in Rutland, VT

    We install Bradford White, Rheem and AO Smith on the majority of our swaps.     Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Rutland, VT

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. The space runs cooler.

    Bradford White Water Heater Services in Rutland

    We use Bradford White a lot on our trucks because the tanks hold up and the parts are easy to find. When replacing one, we match fuel, size and venting, check whether the old warranty still applies, and register the new heater right away. Registration matters.

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Rutland, VT

    Gas water heaters reheat quicker than electric tanks, and in Rutland, VT homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Every joint gets checked.

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A bigger tank or a higher first-hour rating handles more back-to-back showers, and sometimes a 50-gallon high-recovery unit matches a 75 in the same footprint. Recovery rate counts too.
