    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently.     Diverter Valve Repair and Replacement in Rutland, VT

    Water running from the spout during a shower means a failing diverter. It is usually the gate in the spout, or the middle stem on three-handle faucets, and both wear out over time. A new diverter spout takes minutes. Middle stems need a seat too.

    Emergency Tub Faucet Service in Rutland, VT

    Three-Handle to Single-Handle Conversion in Rutland

    Three-handle faucets are worth converting. The old valve comes out, a pressure-balance valve goes in, and a remodel plate covers the extra holes. Most need an access panel or a back-wall opening. Tile around the plate stays intact.
