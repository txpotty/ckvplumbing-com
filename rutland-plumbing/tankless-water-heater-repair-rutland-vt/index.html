    With regular descaling, tankless heaters often last 15 to 20 years. Without maintenance in hard water, problems can start within a couple of years.

    Error codes can be triggered by a variety of issues, such as low water flow, gas supply problems, ignition failures, exhaust blockages or faulty temperature sensors. Each code points to a specific system malfunction.

    In homes with hard water and no softener, once a year is a good rule. Homes with a softener may go longer, but annual inspection is still smart.

    Fluctuating temperatures can come from low flow rates, a dirty inlet filter, a failing flow sensor or scale on the heat exchanger. Crossed hot and cold lines at a fixture can cause it too.
