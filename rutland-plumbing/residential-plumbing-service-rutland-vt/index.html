    Hard water carries dissolved calcium and magnesium. A softener swaps those minerals out through ion exchange, and the difference shows up as scale in water heaters, white crust on faucets, spotty dishes and soap that will not lather well. Soft water is easier on fixtures.

    Every repair comes with a written parts and labor warranty. New fixtures and water heaters also carry manufacturer coverage, and we register it for you, which matters because some water heater makers shorten the tank warranty if the unit is never registered. Keep a photo of the serial plate.

    Soft water and normal pressure prevent a lot of what we see. Grease out of the drains and a yearly pressure test help too, since anything over 80 psi wears out supply hoses and fixtures faster than people expect. A yearly inspection catches the rest.
