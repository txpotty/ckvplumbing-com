    Water Line Leak Detection in Rutland, VT

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. The bill usually tells you first.     Gas Line Leak Locating in Rutland

    You cannot locate a gas leak by smell alone. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe often marks a leak that has been there a while. Utility coordination is included.

    Pool Leak Detection in Rutland

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation probably has a leak. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, use an underwater hydrophone to listen, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain so we can point to the exact fitting or crack.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Rutland

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, while tracer gas catches silent leaks on PEX and PVC.

    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Rutland, VT

    The service line from the meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue. We trace the line with an electronic locator, then use a ground microphone to hear the leak, switching to tracer gas on plastic lines that do not carry sound.

    Slab Leak Detection in Rutland

    Concrete slab homes have supply lines buried in sand below the floor, and a pinhole there can run for months before anyone notices. A warm tile is the classic sign. We pressure test the hot and cold sides separately, then listen through the slab with a ground microphone and confirm with a thermal camera when the leak is on the hot side.
