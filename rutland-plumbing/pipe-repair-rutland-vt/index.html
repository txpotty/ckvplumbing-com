    Buried Line Repair in Rutland

    Buried pipes can be damaged by settling, roots or deep frost. Seasonal camps and vacation homes are often drained and winterized each fall to keep pipes from bursting while the place sits empty.     PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Rutland, VT

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Both have weak points. PEX can fail at crimp rings, older brass fittings or where it was exposed to UV light in an attic, while CPVC turns brittle with age and heat and cracks when bumped, and we use parts and methods made for each pipe type. Brittle CPVC needs a gentle hand.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Rutland

    Galvanized steel and old copper both corrode with age. Brown water is a clue. Low pressure, discolored water and leaks at threaded joints tell us the pipe is closing off from the inside, so we swap the failing pipe for PEX or copper with proper fittings between metals. Pressure comes right back.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Rutland

    Older homes were plumbed with galvanized steel, which rusts from the inside out. Pressure fades over years. Every time a fitting is disturbed, the next one tends to leak, so repairs on galvanized are usually short-term and replacing the run with PEX or copper is the lasting fix. We use dielectric unions at transitions.
