    Common Toilet Repairs in Rutland, VT

    Emergency Toilet Repair in Rutland, VT

    An overflowing bowl in a one-bathroom house is an emergency, and so is a burst supply line spraying water across the floor at full house pressure. Shut the angle stop first. Turn the small oval handle behind the toilet clockwise until it stops, then call us so we can walk you through the rest. We stop the leak on the first visit and fix it for good when parts allow.

    Fill Valve Replacement in Rutland

    Old ballcock valves with a brass arm and a big plastic float are loud and slow to refill, and they tend to stick open when scale builds up in the ports. We replace them with an adjustable modern fill valve from Fluidmaster or Korky and set the height so the critical level mark sits at least an inch above the overflow. It is a twenty-minute job. You will notice the difference the first night, because the tank stops hissing.

    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Rutland, VT

    Older toilets from Eljer, Briggs, Crane and Universal Rundle still turn up in homes built before the 1990s, and their flush valves often take parts nobody stocks anymore. We carry adapters for some of them. For the rest, we will tell you if parts are available or if replacement is the honest answer.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Rutland

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Commercial bowls usually run on flushometer valves rather than tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. Work can be scheduled before opening or after close.

    Pressure-Assist and Dual-Flush Repair in Rutland

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the china that compresses air with incoming water pressure, and the cartridge inside that vessel eventually wears out. You will not find most of these parts at a hardware store. We carry Flushmate cartridges and dual-flush seals for the common TOTO and American Standard models, and we check house pressure, since these units need steady line pressure to flush properly. Low pressure makes them sound weak.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Rutland, VT

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. If the auger comes back clean and the toilet still backs up, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. We run a cable from the cleanout or put a camera in.

    Clogged Toilet Solutions in Rutland, VT

    Paper towels, wipes, dental floss and toys cause most of the repeat clogs we clear, and none of them break down the way toilet paper does. The closet auger usually hooks them and pulls them out. If the toilet clogs again within a week, the problem is further down the line. Then we look at the branch drain and the main sewer, often with a camera.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Rutland

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. Ghost flushing on a sensor valve usually means the eye needs adjusting or cleaning.
