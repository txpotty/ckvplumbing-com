    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Rutland, VT

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Leak Detection and Slab Screening in Rutland

    A spinning water meter with all fixtures off is a clear sign of a hidden leak. We also check for warm floor spots. We test the meter, watch for pressure loss and look for damp baseboards and mildew, and on slab homes we follow up with acoustic and thermal equipment to pinpoint the leak. Early slab leak finds save floors and foundations.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Rutland

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Vermont houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. Each gets tested, not guessed at. Catching a corroded fitting or a partial sewer blockage now keeps it from becoming a ceiling stain or a backup on a holiday weekend.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Rutland, VT

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. We check for old uncoated brass connectors, missing shutoffs, missing sediment traps and corrosion, and we test joints with an electronic detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Pre-Purchase Plumbing Inspections in Rutland

    A house can pass a general inspection and still have a sewer full of roots. Our report tells buyers what pipe the house has, what the sewer looks like and how old the heater is, while there is still time to act. Findings help with negotiations. Old galvanized, a damaged sewer or an aging heater can all become a repair request or a closing credit.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Rutland, VT

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. The camera goes in at a cleanout and travels to the main or the tank, recording roots, cracks, offsets and bellies along the way. We mark problem spots from above. Most Vermont homes have full basements, many with fieldstone or older foundations that can let in water during wet seasons.

    Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Rutland, VT

    Heating systems, boilers and water heaters run hard through the winter, so annual service helps prevent breakdowns in the coldest weeks. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. Outdoor faucets, exposed pipe, irrigation backflow devices and the heater drain and relief valve all get checked. Insulation gets checked too. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Commercial Plumbing Inspections in Rutland, VT

    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Rutland

    High water bills, repeat clogs, slow drains and discolored water are common signs. Banging pipes count too. Knocking pipes, a rumbling heater or mold near fixtures usually mean something underneath needs a professional look.

    Every inspection comes with a written report, photos and sewer video when included. Reports are ready the same day or the next business day.
