    Burst Drain Line Repair in Rutland, VT

    Inside a wall, a broken drain line can soak flooring fast. Stop running water to it. When you call with an active leak, we move the visit up, isolate the damaged section and replace the broken pipe on the first visit in most cases. We tell you about wet drywall right away.

    Bathroom Drain Repair in Rutland

    Hair and soap scum are the main problem in bath drains. Scale speeds it up. We clear sinks, tubs and showers, replace broken pop-up stoppers and worn P-traps, and fix leaking tub shoes and shower drain bodies that let water drip into the ceiling below. Most of these are one-hour jobs.

    Restaurant and Office Drain Repair in Rutland

    Restaurants, salons, offices and medical suites all need working drains during business hours. Kitchens clog the most. We repair grease-laden kitchen lines, floor sinks, mop sink drains and restroom fixtures, working before opening or after close to keep the doors open. A cleaning schedule prevents most of it.

    Broken Drain Pipe Repair in Rutland, VT

    Broken drain pipe usually comes from settling, roots or age. Sometimes it is a bad glue joint. We find the break with a camera and a locator, then replace that section with new pipe joined by shielded couplings that fit the old and new outside diameters. The repair gets a flow test.

    Clogged Drain Repair in Rutland

    Grease, hair, food scraps and mineral scale cause most clogs. Repeat clogs are a warning. We clear the line with a drum auger or a jetter, then camera the pipe when the blockage is deep or keeps coming back, which shows whether you have buildup, a sag or a broken fitting. The footage tells us what to fix.

    Kitchen Branch Line Repair in Rutland

    This branch takes more grease and food than any other line in most homes. Buildup gets deep. We replace cracked tailpieces, fix slip nuts and correct trap height, then jet the branch line and check its fall when the buildup sits past the wall. Dishwasher and disposal connections get checked.

    Drain Vent Repair in Rutland, VT

    Gurgling and slow draining often mean the vent is blocked. Air cannot get in behind the water. Without air, draining water pulls the trap seal along with it and lets sewer gas into the room, so we check the roof vent for nests or debris and look for cracked or disconnected vent pipe in the walls. Many vent fixes take under an hour.
