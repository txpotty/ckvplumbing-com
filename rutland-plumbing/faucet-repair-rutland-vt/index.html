    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Rutland

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. Brand-matched hoses, heads and weights go in, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Rutland, VT

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. A cracked body or discontinued parts change the answer. You get a repair price and a replacement price so the choice is yours.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Rutland, VT

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. A diverter costs very little. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Rutland

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Rutland

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. Anything worn gets replaced, and the rest gets checked. Each valve type, from compression to ceramic disc, wears in its own way, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Rutland, VT

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. A seats and springs kit fixes most of them. Loose handles happen too. If the handle leaks, snugging the adjusting ring under the cap often fixes it.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Rutland, VT

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. The common stems ride on the truck. Filtered water faucets at the kitchen sink get new cartridges or check valves as needed.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Rutland, VT

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.
