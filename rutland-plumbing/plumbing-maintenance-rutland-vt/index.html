    Hot Water Tank Maintenance in Rutland, VT

    Whether gas or electric, your hot water tank benefits greatly from annual maintenance. We check burner operation, flue venting, and potential gas leaks on gas models, and inspect electrical connections and heating elements on electric units. Sediment flushing is a key part of our service. It removes mineral deposits that can reduce heating efficiency and lead to early tank failure.

    Backflow Preventer Testing in Rutland

    Backflow preventers protect the drinking water supply from contamination, and many water providers call for annual testing for irrigation and commercial devices. Our certified testers check each valve and file results with your water utility as required. If a device fails, we repair or replace the internal parts. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Keeping up with testing avoids fines and water shutoffs.

    Preventative Leak Detection in Rutland, VT

    Early leak detection is a key part of our plumbing maintenance. We use advanced tools to find hidden leaks in pipes, especially those under slabs or behind walls, which are common in older homes. Vermont has some of the oldest housing in the country, including farmhouses and village homes built well over a century ago. Catching these leaks early prevents serious water damage, mold growth, and wasted water, saving homeowners in Rutland from extensive repairs down the line.

    Repiping and Polybutylene Replacement in Rutland, VT

    Homes with polybutylene or aging galvanized pipe face a higher risk of leaks and low water pressure. During maintenance, we check pipe materials and look for signs of failure. If repiping makes sense, we explain PEX and copper options and give a written estimate. Repiping can be done in stages to fit your budget. New pipe brings back strong pressure and confidence.

    Gas Line Inspection and Repair in Rutland

    Gas lines feed water heaters, furnaces, ranges, dryers and outdoor grills or fire pits. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections for leaks and check that appliance shutoffs work. Flexible connectors that are old or kinked get swapped out. Any gas repair is done to local code with pressure testing where required. If you ever smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility right away.
