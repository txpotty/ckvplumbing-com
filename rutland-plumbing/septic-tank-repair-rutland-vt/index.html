    Aerobic and Alternative System Repair in Rutland, VT

    Aerobic treatment units use an air pump to feed oxygen to the bacteria, and when that pump dies the unit starts to smell within days.     Drain Field Repair in Rutland, VT

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. Most of the time it is biomat, a slimy layer that seals the trench bottom.
