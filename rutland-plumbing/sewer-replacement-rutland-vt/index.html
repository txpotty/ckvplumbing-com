    Sewer Line Repair vs. Full Replacement in Rutland

    Deciding between repair and full replacement depends on the extent and nature of the damage.     Clay and Orangeburg Pipe Replacement in Rutland, VT

    Clay pipe joints let roots in, while Orangeburg pipe made from tar paper deforms over time. Both are prone to failure. We replace these lines with modern materials. Camera inspection shows the extent of damage. Replacement ends ongoing root problems.

    Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Rutland

    Cast iron sewer pipe can corrode from the inside, creating rough surfaces and holes. Scale buildup snags debris and causes clogs. We replace cast iron with PVC or HDPE. This restores flow and eliminates corrosion. Many homes built before 1975 have cast iron lines.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Rutland

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is reliable for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Rutland homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We carefully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Rutland

    Pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE pipe through the old line while breaking the old pipe apart. It requires only small access pits at each end. This protects landscaping, driveways and patios. It works well for clay, cast iron and Orangeburg pipe. Most jobs are finished in one day.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Rutland, VT

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, simultaneously breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are ideal for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Rutland.

    CKV Plumbing offers comprehensive warranties on both the materials we install and our workmanship for sewer replacement services.     For sewer replacement, we mainly use durable and long-lasting materials like PVC (polyvinyl chloride) and ABS (acrylonitrile butadiene styrene) pipes. We select the best material for your specific property and local conditions.

    Modern PVC and HDPE pipes have tight joints that resist roots. Proper installation greatly reduces risk.
