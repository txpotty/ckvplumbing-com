    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Rutland

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet.     Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Rutland, VT

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.
