    How We Set New Sinks in Rutland, VT

    Drop-In Sink Installation in Rutland, VT

    Drop-in sinks are the most forgiving style. The rim hides small cutout flaws, so laminate tops take a drop-in with just a size check and the right sealant. Clips pull it tight to the counter. Stainless drop-ins weigh very little.

    Undermount Bowl Setting on Stone in Rutland

    Stone shops cut the hole, but we set the bowl. An even reveal around a polished cutout takes shims and patience before the clips are tightened. A crooked reveal never goes away. We check it from both ends of the counter.

    Undermount Clips and Cure Time in Rutland

    Undermount bowls need overnight to cure. The silicone bead under the rim reaches handling strength in a few hours but needs about a day before it carries the weight of a full basin of water and a pot leaning on the edge. The bowl stays braced until our return. Plumbing goes in on the second visit.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Rutland, VT

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. The backsplash gets sealed last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Kitchen Sink Installation in Rutland

    Kitchen sinks come in four mounts: drop-in, undermount, farmhouse and flush-set. Each one needs its own support, and a cast iron farmhouse sink can weigh well over a hundred pounds before it holds any water, so the cabinet often needs reinforcement first. The cabinet gets checked before install day. Disposal and dishwasher connections are part of the job.

    Sink Replacement and Upgrades in Rutland

    Upgrading the sink is a cheap way to refresh a room. Swapping a chipped drop-in for a new one in the same cutout usually takes a morning, and moving up to a deeper single bowl may only need a new trap and a short drain extension. Countertop changes add time. We tell you which one you are looking at.

    Leak-Proof Sink Connections in Rutland, VT

    Every joint gets tested before we leave. The bowl gets filled and drained while we watch every nut under the sink with a light and a dry towel. Paper towels catch the smallest drip. Pipe dope goes on threads, not compression fittings.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Rutland

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Glue alone is not enough. Plumbing goes on the next day.
