    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Rutland, VT

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Rutland, VT

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Urgent Water Softener Repair in Rutland, VT

    Major leaks and total system failure call for a fast response. CKV Plumbing handles urgent softener calls in Rutland, VT ahead of scheduled maintenance. We bypass the unit so the house keeps water, then repair the valve, the tank or the fitting that failed and caused the leak. Bypass first.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Rutland, VT

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Control Valve Repair in Rutland

    Water that runs to the drain all day usually means the control valve is stuck between cycles. CKV Plumbing rebuilds stuck valves with the correct seal and spacer kit for your model.     Emergency Water Softener Repair in Rutland

    A softener stuck in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours or flood a utility room. We handle these calls quickly.     Resin Testing and Replacement in Rutland

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Treated city water slowly breaks resin down into mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Rutland

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. Older timer units regenerate on a schedule no matter how much water you used. We retest hardness and reprogram the valve, or upgrade the head to a metered control that only regenerates when the resin is actually close to exhausted. Salt bills drop.
