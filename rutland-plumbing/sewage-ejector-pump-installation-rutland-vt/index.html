    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Rutland

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps remove water, their applications are different. CKV Plumbing provides clear guidance and installation for both in Rutland. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, usually at a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks and showers in below-grade plumbing. We make sure you get the correct pump for your drainage needs, preventing costly mistakes.
