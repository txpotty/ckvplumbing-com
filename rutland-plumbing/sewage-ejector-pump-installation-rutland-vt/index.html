    Ejector Pump Jobs We Handle in Rutland, VT

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Rutland, VT

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars produce gray water that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Lint kills small pumps.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Rutland

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. They cost more. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Zoeller Ejector Pump Installation in Rutland, VT

    Zoeller has made ejector pumps for a very long time, and its cast iron models are the ones we see still running in older basements. We install Zoeller for both new basins and drop-in replacements, and its standard models often fit existing pits and discharge piping without changes. Floats and switches are stocked locally.

    Emergency Ejector Pump Replacement in Rutland, VT

    A dead ejector pump means the basement fixtures stop working until it is fixed. Quit using the basement fixtures and the washer if it feeds the pit, then call (802) 209-5139 and we aim to have a plumber and pump at your door the same day. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Rutland

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. A sump pump moves groundwater from a perimeter drain to the yard, while an ejector moves sewage from toilets and sinks to the sewer, and the ejector basin has to be sealed and vented because of the gas. Never mix the two.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Rutland

    Residential ejector systems in Rutland serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. The parts are simple. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Rutland, VT

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit. If the basin is sound, we can swap in a better pump, replace the old swing check with a new one, add a full-port shutoff and install a high water alarm, which covers most of what a new system offers for a fraction of the cost. We tell you if the basin is done.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Basin & Pit Installation in Rutland

    Digging the pit means cutting the slab and excavating below it, deep enough to set the basin flush with the finished floor. Rocky glacial soil and ledge are common across Vermont, which can make digging for water lines, sewer repairs or septic work slow and costly. We bed the basin on gravel and backfill around it so it cannot float when groundwater rises, then pour new concrete to seal the floor and lock the basin in place. A floating basin breaks pipe.
