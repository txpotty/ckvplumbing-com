    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in St. Johnsbury, VT

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in St. Johnsbury, VT, a pressure vacuum breaker, or PVB, is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by letting air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers carefully install PVBs above the highest point of downstream piping, as code requires, to maintain effective protection. We also make sure of proper clearance and drainage.

    Commercial Backflow Prevention in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Commercial properties in St. Johnsbury, VT often have complex plumbing systems that pose a higher risk of cross-contamination and require robust backflow prevention. Restaurants, hospitals, industrial facilities and even multi-family buildings must comply with commercial plumbing codes. CKV Plumbing provides complete installation of heavy-duty backflow preventers, including large diameter RPZ assemblies, to safeguard public health and meet Vermont regulations for businesses.     Backflow Testing and Certification in St. Johnsbury

    Most water providers require testable backflow assemblies to be certified at installation and then once a year. Our certified testers check each valve's opening point, relief valve operation and differential pressure. If a device fails, we can repair or rebuild it right away with genuine parts. We submit passing results so you avoid compliance letters or service interruption.

    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Device Installation in St. Johnsbury, VT

    For high-hazard applications in St. Johnsbury, VT where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a reduced pressure zone, or RPZ, device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, using a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. CKV Plumbing specializes in RPZ installation, making sure all components are correctly sized, positioned and set up according to state and local plumbing codes. Proper installation is essential for effective operation.

    Double Check Valve Assembly Installation in St. Johnsbury

    Double check assemblies are used for low hazard connections such as many fire sprinkler systems and some irrigation lines. They have two spring-loaded check valves and test cocks for annual certification. Unlike an RPZ, they do not discharge water, so they can be installed in vaults or below grade where local rules allow. We size them to avoid too much pressure loss on your system.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in St. Johnsbury

    The double check valve assembly, or DCVA, is a common backflow preventer for low-hazard applications where the consequences of backflow are not severe. In St. Johnsbury, DCVAs are frequently installed on fire sprinkler systems or irrigation lines. Our certified plumbers precisely install these devices, making sure both check valves operate correctly and are properly sealed to prevent backpressure or back-siphonage into the main water supply.

    Backflow Prevention for Irrigation Systems in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Irrigation systems in St. Johnsbury, VT are a frequent source of backflow, as stagnant water and lawn chemicals can be drawn back into the potable water supply. Installing a dedicated backflow preventer for your sprinkler system is often a local code requirement. We specialize in selecting and installing the correct device, such as a pressure vacuum breaker or atmospheric vacuum breaker, to protect your home's main water line from contaminants coming from your landscape irrigation.

    Backflow Enclosures and Freeze Protection in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Above-ground assemblies sit in direct sun in summer and can freeze on cold nights. Keeping heat on, insulating vulnerable lines and letting faucets drip during extreme cold are standard habits for homeowners here. We install insulated enclosures, freeze blankets or heated cabinets based on your location. Enclosures also discourage theft, since brass backflow assemblies are a common target for scrap thieves. We choose enclosures with enough room for testing and service.
