    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in St. Johnsbury

    PEX Pipe Replacement in St. Johnsbury, VT

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.
