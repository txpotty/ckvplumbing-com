    Frequently Asked Questions About Faucet Repair in St. Johnsbury

    Most faucet repairs cost $125 to $300, including the cartridge or seals and labor. Complex kitchen or touchless faucet repairs may run $200 to $400. We quote a flat price before any work starts.

    We repair Moen, Delta, Kohler, Pfister, American Standard, Grohe, Hansgrohe and nearly all other brands. We carry common cartridges on the truck.

    Most repairs take 30 to 60 minutes. If a special part must be ordered, we might need a second short visit.

    A worn cartridge, washer or valve seat is the usual culprit. Hard water scale makes these parts wear out faster, so drips are common in many homes.

    If the faucet body is solid and parts are available, a repair is generally worth it. If it is corroded, cracked or discontinued, a new faucet is often the better choice.

    Common signs include a persistent drip or leak, difficulty turning the handle, low water pressure, strange noises when the faucet is on, or visible corrosion around the base or spout. If you notice any of these issues, it's best to address them promptly to prevent further damage or water waste.

    Many brands, including Moen and Delta, offer limited lifetime warranties on cartridges and finishes for the original owner. We can help you check whether parts might be covered.

    Low water pressure can stem from several issues, including mineral deposits clogging the aerator or internal components, a worn-out cartridge, issues with your home's main water supply, or even problems with municipal water pressure.
