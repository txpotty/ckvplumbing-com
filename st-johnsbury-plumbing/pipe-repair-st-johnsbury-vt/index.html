    Galvanized Pipe Repair in St. Johnsbury

    Threaded steel pipe rusts from the inside until flow drops and threaded joints start to leak. It can be patched. The trouble is that every fitting you disturb on old galvanized can start a new leak, which is why we use dielectric transitions and lay out a replacement plan for the rest. You decide the timing.

    Pipe Repair vs Repipe Cost in St. Johnsbury, VT

    A single pipe repair costs far less than a whole-house repipe. It makes sense on sound pipe. If you are seeing repeated leaks, low pressure or rusty water, the total cost of patching can pass the cost of repiping within a few years, so we lay out both options with real numbers and tell you which one we would pick for our own house. You make the call.

    Underground Pipe Repair in St. Johnsbury

    Buried pipe takes stress from settling soil, roots and vehicles. You rarely see them. We locate underground leaks with acoustic equipment and tracer gas, and either spot repair at the leak or replace the line by pulling new pipe through. Every repair gets a pressure test.

    PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in St. Johnsbury

    PEX is flexible and handles freezing better than rigid pipe. It still fails. Most PEX leaks happen at a crimp ring that was not fully seated, an early brass fitting that dezincified or a section damaged by sunlight in an attic, and we cut out the bad fitting and replace it with a crimp, clamp or expansion connection rated for the pipe. Most take under an hour.
