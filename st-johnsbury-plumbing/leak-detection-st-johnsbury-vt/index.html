    Slab Leaks From Ground Movement in St. Johnsbury

    Pipe set in concrete cannot flex when the ground moves, so it cracks at elbows and where it rises through the slab. Rocky glacial soil and ledge are common across Vermont, which can make digging for water lines, sewer repairs or septic work slow and costly. We isolate the line first. Then acoustic listening and thermal imaging find the leak without breaking up the floor to look, and you get a marked spot plus repair options before any concrete gets cut.

    PEX and Copper Leak Detection in St. Johnsbury, VT

    On a ground microphone, copper leaks are easy to hear. PEX is quieter, since plastic pipe does not carry sound well, so we lean more on thermal scans, moisture readings and tracer gas when the house is plumbed in it. Both show up on our schedule every week. Pinholes in copper and failed crimp fittings in PEX are the two leaks we find most.

    Pool and Spa Leak Detection in St. Johnsbury

    Heat and wind can pull a lot of water out of a pool, which makes it tricky to separate evaporation from a real leak. A bucket test settles it. We then pressure test the plumbing lines and dye test around lights, skimmers and return fittings, because a running leak can undermine the soil around the pool.

    Pool Leak Detection in St. Johnsbury

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, listen for leaks with a hydrophone underwater, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain to find the exact crack or fitting that is losing water.

    Hidden Wall and Ceiling Leaks in St. Johnsbury

    Bubbling paint, soft drywall and a musty smell are the signs we hear about most. All of them mean water. Thermal cameras find the wet area, and a moisture meter confirms it. Acoustic tools help when the leak is on a pressurized line, and once the source is pinned down we open one small access hole rather than following the stain around the room.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in St. Johnsbury
