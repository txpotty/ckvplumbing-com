    Waterproofing Costs vs. Repeat Water Damage in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Paying once beats paying every two years. Waterproofing a shower wall usually lands in the low thousands, while repeat drywall, cabinet and floor repairs add up fast, and mold remediation on top of that can double the bill. The price for exterior block sealing depends on how much wall there is. We write up each option with its price so you can compare.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in St. Johnsbury

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. The bad fitting or section of pipe gets replaced. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Exterior Block Wall Waterproofing in St. Johnsbury, VT

    White crust on block or stem walls is efflorescence, salt left behind as water moves through the masonry and dries. The usual cause is a sprinkler head spraying the wall instead of the plants, or soil graded toward the foundation. We fix the water first. After that the wall gets cleaned, dried and coated with a breathable masonry sealer or elastomeric coating.

    Sump Pump Installation and Repair in St. Johnsbury, VT

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized for the inflow, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in St. Johnsbury

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing so water has somewhere to go. Clay soils in river valleys drain poorly and can hold water against foundations, so sump systems are common in low-lying homes. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Foundation Crack Repair in St. Johnsbury

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin starting at the lowest port and working up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Below-Grade and Basement Wall Waterproofing in St. Johnsbury

    Older homes often still have galvanized supply lines, cast iron drains or even lead service lines that warrant inspection and replacement. Any wall below grade sits in soil that gets saturated after heavy rain or snowmelt, and that water pushes against the wall with real hydrostatic pressure. We inject cracks, coat the wall inside or out, and add drain tile wherever water collects at the footing. A sump goes in if groundwater sits high. Not every basement needs one.
