    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in St. Johnsbury, VT

    The float switch is a key component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in St. Johnsbury, VT.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in St. Johnsbury

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We confirm these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your St. Johnsbury residence.

    Crawlspace and Window Well Pumps in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Not every sump sits in a basement. Many homes with crawlspaces or deep window wells use small pumps to clear runoff.     Commercial and Elevator Pit Pumps in St. Johnsbury

    Commercial buildings often have sump pumps in elevator pits, parking garages and mechanical rooms. These pumps may need oil-minder controls or alarms to meet code and insurance requirements.     Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in St. Johnsbury

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, gravel and leaves settle in the pit. That debris can get sucked into the intake screen and jam the impeller. We pump down and scoop out the basin, rinse the intake and inspect the pit liner for cracks. A clean basin helps the pump run cooler and last longer.
