    Overflow and Drain Body Leak Repair in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Water that appears only when the sink is full often comes from the overflow gasket or the drain flange putty. We pull the drain, clean the old putty or sealant, and reset the flange with fresh plumber's putty or silicone based on the basin material.     Pop-Up Drain and Stopper Repair in St. Johnsbury, VT

    When the stopper will not close, will not open or lets water slowly escape, the pop-up linkage is typically to blame. If the drain body itself is corroded, we replace it with a matching finish.
