    Commercial Water Heater Repair and Installation in St. Johnsbury, VT

    A commercial water heater is judged by recovery rate, the gallons it can reheat per hour, not by tank size alone. Undersized heaters run cold by mid-shift. Our techs work on AO Smith, Rheem, Bradford White and State commercial heaters and check flue draft, gas valves and the T and P valve each visit. New installs get sized from your peak hour demand. That is how a busy kitchen stops running out of hot water during the dinner push.

    After-Hours Commercial Plumbing in St. Johnsbury

    Emergencies at a business cost more the longer they last, so speed matters more than the hourly rate. Water on the floor comes first.     Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in St. Johnsbury

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Clay soils in river valleys drain poorly and can hold water against foundations, so sump systems are common in low-lying homes. Meter and pressure tests confirm it, and acoustic gear plus thermal imaging narrow the spot down to a foot or so. Then we decide. A spot repair through the floor makes sense for some, and an overhead reroute makes more sense for others. You get a price on each option.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in St. Johnsbury

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. A jetted line stays open far longer than a cabled one. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Big tank heaters fail in a few common ways: a failed gas valve, a cracked flue baffle, a stuck T and P valve or a tank full of scale. Each sounds different. Our techs test flue draft, check combustion, measure the temperature at the farthest fixture and flush sediment, which together tell us whether to repair the unit or plan its replacement. Tankless units get a descaling flush. If the building has a recirc loop, we test the pump and check valve too.

    Commercial Restroom Fixture Repair in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Restroom calls are the most common commercial work we do. Running flush valves, stuck urinals and dripping sensor faucets usually need a diaphragm, a solenoid or fresh batteries. We stock those. Wall-hung toilets that keep clogging or rocking get a carrier check, since a loose bowl can leak inside the wall. Low pressure on one fixture usually points to a clogged supply stop.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in St. Johnsbury

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers are routine work for us. ADA work is part of it too. For ADA remodels we set fixture heights, add grab bar blocking in the wall and insulate the drain and supply lines under sinks.
