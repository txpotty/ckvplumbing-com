    What Fails on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Emergency Disposal and Sink Backup Repair in St. Johnsbury

    When a disposal fails, water can back into the sink and the dishwasher. If water is rising or reaching the floor, shut off the disposal and the dishwasher and call us, since running either one just adds more water to the cabinet. We respond fast.

    Humming Disposal Repair in St. Johnsbury

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Bones, pits, glass and spoons are the usual suspects. With the power off, we free the flywheel using the hex socket underneath, pull the object, and test the motor and reset with water running.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in St. Johnsbury

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough usually mean trouble inside the unit. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in St. Johnsbury

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. The red reset button on the bottom is the first stop, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. In that damp cabinet, wire nuts corrode and cord connections loosen, so we replace them instead of retightening.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in St. Johnsbury, VT

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. It is not a repairable part.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in St. Johnsbury

    A disposal that hums but will not grind is getting power but is stuck, while one that is silent has no power at all. They are different problems. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard cause most disposal smells. Town water systems in St. Johnsbury and other communities are generally reliable, but older service lines can add rust and discolored water. Mineral scale traps the food. We clean the splash guard and chamber, check the drain for buildup, and replace a worn guard, which often clears up a smell that months of lemon peels never touched.

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in St. Johnsbury

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps wedge between the impeller lugs and the grind ring, and the motor stalls. We shut off power, insert a 1/4 inch hex key into the socket on the bottom of the unit and work it back and forth until the flywheel turns freely. Next we pull the object with pliers. Long stalls can overheat the motor, so we run it under load before we finish.
