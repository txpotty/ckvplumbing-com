    Frequently Asked Questions About Backflow Preventer in St. Johnsbury

    Annual residential backflow testing typically costs $75 to $150 per assembly. Repairs with a rebuild kit usually run $150 to $450. New residential installation ranges from $300 to $1,200, and commercial RPZ assemblies often cost $1,500 to $5,000 installed.

    Backflow preventers stop contaminated water from flowing backward into the public supply during pressure drops. Irrigation, fire lines and commercial equipment are frequent sources of contamination. Water providers require them to protect public health.

    Backflow prevention is essential because of changing water pressures, main breaks and fire flow that can drop pressure suddenly, and the potential for cross-connections from irrigation systems or industrial processes. These factors increase the risk of contaminated water entering the public supply, making a working backflow preventer a must.

    CKV Plumbing services all major types of backflow preventers, including Reduced Pressure Zone Assemblies, Double Check Valve Assemblies, Pressure Vacuum Breakers, and Atmospheric Vacuum Breakers. We work with leading brands like Watts, Wilkins, Febco, and Conbraco.

    If your backflow preventer fails its annual test, you will typically receive a notice from the St. Johnsbury water department requiring prompt repair or replacement. Our technicians can diagnose the failure, perform the necessary repairs, and retest the device so it passes inspection and avoids any service interruptions or penalties.
