    Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Plumbing Service in St. Johnsbury

    Small repairs in St. Johnsbury typically cost $150 to $300. A main line clog or a new water heater falls more in the $400 to $1,500 range, sometimes higher. We quote after we look. You get it in writing, and it only changes if you approve.

    A slab leak is a break in a water line running under a concrete foundation. Clay soils in river valleys drain poorly and can hold water against foundations, so sump systems are common in low-lying homes. Warm spots on the floor, a sudden jump in the water bill or the sound of water running with every fixture off are the usual signs. Do not wait on it.

    Watch for drips that will not stop, slow drains, banging or whistling pipes, a sudden drop in pressure, discolored water and wet spots with no obvious source. Any one of these points to a problem behind the wall or under the floor. Do not ignore them.

    Gurgling is the early one. After that come slow drains, a sewer smell and water rising in the shower pan, and when more than one fixture acts up at the same time, the clog is usually in the main line rather than a single trap. Main line clogs rarely clear on their own.

    Emergencies go to the top of the board. Our goal is a licensed plumber at your door within a few hours, and for a burst pipe or sewage backup we will walk you through closing the main shutoff on the phone so the damage stops while we drive. Grab towels and a bucket while you wait.

    Once a year is a good rule. Hard water speeds up sediment buildup, so homes with scale may need a flush more often, and a yearly check lets us test the relief valve, look at the anode rod and catch a weeping fitting before it rusts through the jacket. An anode rod often wears out in three to five years.

    Hard water carries dissolved calcium and magnesium. A softener swaps those minerals out through ion exchange, and the difference shows up as scale in water heaters, white crust on faucets, spotty dishes and soap that will not lather well. Fixtures last longer on soft water.

    Our plumbers install and service Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Rheem, Bradford White and Navien, among others. We can point you to the models that hold up best with your water. Some cost more but last longer.
