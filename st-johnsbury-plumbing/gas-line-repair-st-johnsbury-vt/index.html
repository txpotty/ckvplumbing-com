    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. New coated connectors go in. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Gas Line Extensions and Relocations in St. Johnsbury

    Adding a gas fireplace, moving a laundry room or switching a range from electric to gas usually means extending the gas line. Sizing comes first. We add up the BTU load of every appliance on the system, measure the longest run and pick a pipe size that keeps pressure up at the farthest appliance when everything runs together. Then we route it, tie it in and test it.

    Buried Yard Line Repair and Replacement in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Many older homes have steel gas lines running from the meter to the house or out to a pool heater. The wrap fails eventually. Once the coating cracks, the pipe starts to corrode, and wet soil from sprinklers or a low spot in the yard speeds that up a great deal. We call 811 before digging. The new line is polyethylene with tracer wire, or coated steel where it has to be exposed, and we backfill and clean up the yard when the test passes.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in St. Johnsbury, VT

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. A whole-house tankless heater can draw 150,000 BTU or more, and that often means upsizing the gas line all the way back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Natural Gas Leak Detection and Repair in St. Johnsbury

    Finding a gas leak starts at the meter. With every appliance off, a moving test dial on the meter tells us gas is escaping somewhere, and then we isolate branches to narrow it down. An electronic detector does the rest. A spray of soap solution on the fitting shows bubbles at the leak, and we fix that joint or section and retest the whole system.

    After-Hours Gas Line Repair in St. Johnsbury

    Gas problems do not wait for business hours, and losing heat or hot water overnight is a real problem. We answer late calls. After you and your family are outside and the utility has made the scene safe, our plumber comes out to locate the leak, make the repair and run the pressure test. We coordinate with the utility on the meter.
