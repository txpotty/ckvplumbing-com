    Pinhole Leak Detection in St. Johnsbury, VT

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause serious damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We employ non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots that indicate water. Our plumbers are adept at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth and compromised structural integrity. Short summers with occasional humidity bring condensation on cold pipes in basements, which can cause drips and mold if left alone.

    Leak Detection for PEX and Copper Pipes in St. Johnsbury

    Whether your plumbing system uses modern PEX tubing or traditional copper pipes, CKV Plumbing has the expertise to detect leaks in both materials.     Slab Leak Detection in St. Johnsbury

    Slab leaks are common in homes on shifting soil and moving foundations. These leaks occur in pipes beneath your concrete slab, often signaled by warm spots on the floor, sudden spikes in your water bill or the sound of running water when all fixtures are off. CKV Plumbing uses specialized acoustic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to precisely locate these hidden leaks without destructive guesswork. Early detection is key to preventing major foundation damage.

    Yard and Outdoor Leak Detection in St. Johnsbury

Detecting these outdoor leaks early can also prevent erosion and potential foundation issues caused by saturated soil, a critical concern for homes in St. Johnsbury, VT.
